# Versorgungswerke — the pension system for the Kammerberufe

Everyone in this repo pays into the **Deutsche Rentenversicherung** except one group, and
that group contains several of its best-paid professions. Members of the
**Kammerberufe** — doctors, dentists, vets, pharmacists, lawyers, notaries, architects, tax
advisers, psychotherapists — are generally **exempt from the statutory pension and belong to
their profession's own Versorgungswerk instead.**

The repo already profiles [Arzt](../jobs/healthcare/arzt-aerztin.md) and
[Apotheker](../jobs/healthcare/apotheker-in.md), describes the ladders to Steuerberater and
Rechtsanwalt, and adds [Architekt](../jobs/engineering/architekt-in.md) — and until now
mentioned this nowhere. It matters most to exactly the people this repo is written for:
anyone arriving mid-career, and anyone who may leave Germany again.

## What it is

A **Versorgungswerk** is a compulsory occupational pension institution attached to a
professional chamber, established under **state** law. Join the chamber, join the
Versorgungswerk. There are dozens — generally one per profession per Bundesland, sometimes
shared across several states.

| | **Deutsche Rentenversicherung** | **Versorgungswerk** |
|---|---|---|
| Financing | Pay-as-you-go (Umlage) | **Funded** — contributions are invested |
| Membership | Employment-based | **Profession-based**, via the Kammer |
| Contribution | Fixed rate up to the ceiling | Usually pegged to the same rate and ceiling |
| Employer share | Half | **Half — the employer must pay it** (§172a SGB VI) |
| Typical outcome | Modest | **Materially higher** for the same career |
| Insolvency backstop | The federal government | None comparable — state supervision only |
| Covers | Old age, reduced earning capacity, survivors | The same three, and **Berufsunfähigkeit cover is often better** |

The reduced-earning-capacity point is worth separating out. The statutory system pays
**Erwerbsminderungsrente**, which asks whether you can do *any* work. Most Versorgungswerke
insure **Berufsunfähigkeit** — whether you can still practise *your profession*. For a
surgeon with a hand injury or a dentist with a back, that is a different question with a
different answer.

## The exemption is applied for, not automatic

**§6 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 SGB VI.** If you are *employed* in a Kammerberuf, you are in the statutory
system by default and must **apply** to be exempted:

1. Join the Kammer and the Versorgungswerk.
2. File the Befreiungsantrag through the Versorgungswerk, which passes it to the DRV.
3. **Within three months** of starting the employment, or the exemption runs only from the
   date of application, leaving a gap paid into the wrong system.

**The exemption is tied to the specific job, not to you.** Following case law, it must be
**re-applied for on every change of employer**, and on a material change of role with the
same one. People who assume it carries over discover the gap years later.

Self-employed practice is different: outside the statutory system anyway for most of these
professions, so the Versorgungswerk simply is the pension.

## Contributions in practice

Most Versorgungswerke set the standard contribution at the **statutory rate applied to the
statutory ceiling**, so an employed member pays roughly what a DRV member on the same salary
would — half of it from the employer. Beyond that:

- **Voluntary over-payment** is usually permitted, often up to a multiple of the standard
  contribution, and is the main reason Versorgungswerk pensions end up higher.
- Contributions count as **Altersvorsorgeaufwendungen** for tax, like DRV contributions —
  see [tax and net pay](taxes-and-net-pay.md).
- Reduced contributions are normal in the first years after qualification, and while in
  parental leave or a Weiterbildung post.

## Moving: the three cases that go wrong

**1. Between Bundesländer.** A different state means a different Versorgungswerk. Most have
**Überleitungsabkommen** that transfer the accrued entitlement — but typically only if you
apply **within a short window** (commonly six months) of joining the new one. Miss it and
you keep a frozen fragment in the old one and start again in the new. This is the single
most common avoidable loss, and it affects doctors above all, who move states for
Weiterbildung posts.

**2. Between systems, inside Germany.** Leave the profession — a doctor moving into industry
or administration — and you stop contributing to the Versorgungswerk and resume DRV
contributions. Nothing transfers. You end with two part-pensions, which is fine, **unless
neither reaches its own minimum qualifying period.** The DRV needs **five years** of
contributions before it pays anything at all, and Versorgungswerk years do not count toward
it. A career split 4/25 can leave four years' worth simply unpayable.

**3. Leaving Germany.** DRV contributions can, for non-EU nationals, be **refunded** after a
waiting period. **Versorgungswerke generally do not work that way.** The usual options are:

- leave the entitlement **beitragsfrei** and draw it at retirement from abroad — normally
  the best outcome, and the one to default to
- **continue voluntarily** from abroad, which several permit
- a **partial refund** of your own contributions, where the statutes allow it, commonly
  after a waiting period and often excluding the employer's half

EU coordination (Reg. 883/2004) does cover Versorgungswerke, so periods count for
*entitlement* elsewhere in the EU — but the practical handling is thinner than for the DRV,
and outside the EU the bilateral social-security treaties frequently do not mention them at
all. **Check before you assume a treaty covers you.**

Whatever you do: **keep the membership number and the annual statement**, and tell the
Versorgungswerk your address abroad. Entitlements are not paid to people who cannot be found.

| Profession | Versorgungswerk |
|---|---|
| [Arzt / Ärztin](../jobs/healthcare/arzt-aerztin.md) | Ärzteversorgung — the largest and oldest group |
| [Apotheker/in](../jobs/healthcare/apotheker-in.md) | Apothekerversorgung |
| [Zahnarzt / Zahnärztin](../jobs/healthcare/zahnarzt-zahnaerztin.md) | Zahnärzteversorgung |
| [Psychotherapeut/in](../jobs/healthcare/psychotherapeut-in.md) | Via the Psychotherapeutenkammer; arrangements vary by state |
| [Architekt/in](../jobs/engineering/architekt-in.md) | Architektenversorgung in most states; joint schemes in others |
| **Rechtsanwalt/-anwältin**, **Notar/in** | Rechtsanwaltsversorgung — reachable from [Rechtsanwaltsfachangestellte/r](../jobs/commercial/rechtsanwaltsfachangestellte-r.md) |
| **Steuerberater/in** | Steuerberaterversorgung — reachable from [Steuerfachangestellte/r](../jobs/commercial/steuerfachangestellte-r.md) |

Not every Kammerberuf has one in every Bundesland, and **Wirtschaftsprüfer** are a partial
case — many are covered through the Steuerberater scheme rather than one of their own.
Confirm with the chamber of the state you will actually practise in.

## Checklist on arrival

1. Register with the **Kammer** — this is not optional and precedes everything.
2. Register with the **Versorgungswerk**.
3. File the **§6 Befreiungsantrag within three months** of starting work.
4. **Re-file it on every job change.**
5. On moving Bundesland, apply for the **Überleitung** immediately.
6. Keep the annual statement. It is the only record you will have if you leave.
