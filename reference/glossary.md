# Glossary

This repo is written in English and deliberately keeps German terms in place, because that
is how you will meet them — in job adverts, on authority websites, and on forms. Around 250
recur across the files. These are the ones that carry meaning.

**If you read only ten:** [Ausbildung](#ausbildung), [Geselle](#geselle),
[Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief), [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b),
[Kammer](#kammer), [Anerkennung](#anerkennung), [Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung](#gleichwertigkeitsprüfung),
[Tarifvertrag](#tarifvertrag), [Beamter](#beamter--beamtin), [brutto / netto](#brutto--netto).

---

### Abschlussprüfung
The final examination of an [Ausbildung](#ausbildung). Modern occupations use a
**gestreckte Abschlussprüfung** — split into Teil 1 partway through and Teil 2 at the end,
with Teil 1 counting toward the final grade. See [ausbildung.md](ausbildung.md).

### Altgesellenregelung
**§7b HwO.** Lets a [Geselle](#geselle) with six years in an [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b)
trade — four of them in a leading position — run a business **without a
[Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief)**. Excludes the health-related trades. The most underused route
in the Handwerk. See [meister.md](meister.md).

### Anerkennung
Recognition of a foreign qualification. Mandatory before working in a regulated profession;
otherwise needed only for the [visa](visa-routes.md). See
[recognition-authorities.md](recognition-authorities.md).

### Anerkennungspartnerschaft
**§16d(3) AufenthG**, since 2024. Lets you enter Germany with **A2** German and a contract,
and pursue recognition after arrival. Used mainly in
[nursing](../jobs/healthcare/pflegefachfrau-pflegefachmann.md).

### Anlage A / Anlage B
The two schedules of the [Handwerksordnung](#handwerksordnung). **Anlage A** trades require a
[Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief) to run a business ([Meisterpflicht](#meisterpflicht)); Anlage B
trades do not. Not static — twelve trades were **re-regulated in 2020**.

### Anpassungslehrgang
A supervised adaptation period, typically 6–12 months, used to close gaps found in a
[Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung](#gleichwertigkeitsprüfung). The alternative is a
[Kenntnisprüfung](#kenntnisprüfung) — the course is safer, the exam faster.

### Anwärter/in
Someone in a [Vorbereitungsdienst](#vorbereitungsdienst), appointed as a **Beamter auf
Widerruf** and paid **Anwärterbezüge**, well below the eventual salary.

### Approbation
The full, permanent licence to practise as a doctor, pharmacist, dentist or psychotherapist.
Contrast [Berufserlaubnis](#berufserlaubnis).

### Arbeitszeugnis
A written reference given on leaving a job, in a coded formal register where bland phrasing
carries grading. Have a German speaker read yours.

### Ausbildung
The dual vocational training system: employed by a company, schooled at a
[Berufsschule](#berufsschule), 2–3½ years, paid throughout. The backbone of German
qualification. See [ausbildung.md](ausbildung.md).

### Auszubildende/r — Azubi
A trainee. The legal term since 1969. **[Lehrling](#lehrling)** is the historical word.

### Ausnahmebewilligung
**§8 HwO.** A discretionary permission to run an [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b) business
without a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief), on documented experience.

### Beamter / Beamtin
A civil servant — appointed under public law, not employed under contract. Paid
[Besoldung](#besoldung), pensioned by the state, **no right to strike**, and generally
restricted to EU/EEA/Swiss nationals. See [beamte-vs-angestellte.md](beamte-vs-angestellte.md).

### Beihilfe
The state's contribution to a [Beamter's](#beamter--beamtin) medical costs, commonly 50–80%.
They insure only the remainder privately. A major reason Beamte net more than their gross
suggests. See [health-insurance.md](health-insurance.md).

### Bereitschaftsdienst
On-call **at the workplace**, paid at a reduced rate. Distinct from
[Rufbereitschaft](#rufbereitschaft).

### Berufserlaubnis
A temporary, restricted permission to practise — usually limited in time, place and employer
— granted while a doctor or pharmacist works toward [Approbation](#approbation).

### Berufsschule
The school half of an [Ausbildung](#ausbildung). **Taught and examined in German, without
exception** — which is why the Ausbildung route requires B1–B2.

### Besoldung
[Beamten](#beamter--beamtin) pay. Grades **A** (ordinary careers), **B** (senior), **R**
(judges), **W** (professors), rising by **Erfahrungsstufen**. **State law since 2006**, so
there are effectively seventeen systems.

### Betriebsrat
The works council. Common in larger firms and genuinely influential over conditions,
monitoring and on-call.

### brutto / netto
Gross and net. Every figure in this repo is **brutto**. Net is commonly 60–65% of it for an
employee. See [taxes-and-net-pay.md](taxes-and-net-pay.md).

### Entgeltgruppe
A pay grade within a [Tarifvertrag](#tarifvertrag) — E, P (nursing), S (social and
education), N (paramedics). Combined with a **Stufe** (experience step) it fixes your salary.

### Fachrichtung / Schwerpunkt / Einsatzgebiet
Specialisations within an occupation. A **Fachrichtung** is certified and printed on your
qualification; an **Einsatzgebiet** is defined by your employer and is not. The difference
matters — compare [Zerspanungs-](../jobs/industrial/zerspanungsmechaniker-in.md) and
[Industriemechaniker](../jobs/industrial/industriemechaniker-in.md).

### Fachsprachprüfung
A profession-specific German exam at the relevant [Kammer](#kammer), required of
third-country [doctors](../jobs/healthcare/arzt-aerztin.md) and
[pharmacists](../jobs/healthcare/apotheker-in.md). **The usual failure point** — not clinical
knowledge.

### Geselle / Gesellin
A qualified tradesperson who has passed the **Gesellenprüfung**, the journeyman's
examination. The rung below [Meister](#meisterbrief).

### Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung
The comparison of your foreign training against the German reference qualification. Outcome:
full equivalence, partial with stated gaps, or none. (**Gleichwertigkeitsfeststellung** is
the same procedure for unregulated occupations.)

### Handwerk
The skilled trades, regulated by the [Handwerksordnung](#handwerksordnung) through the
**Handwerkskammer (HWK)**. Distinct from industry, which answers to the [IHK](#ihk).

### Handwerksordnung — HwO
The law governing the trades, including [Anlage A / B](#anlage-a--anlage-b) and the
[Handwerksrolle](#handwerksrolle).

### Handwerksrolle
The register of Handwerk businesses. Entry is required to run one, and normally means a
[Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief) — or [§7b](#altgesellenregelung), [§8](#ausnahmebewilligung),
§9 for EU nationals, or an employed Betriebsleiter.

### IHK
**Industrie- und Handelskammer** — the chamber for industrial, commercial, IT, logistics and
hospitality occupations. **IHK FOSA** handles foreign-qualification equivalence for them.

### Kammer
A self-governing chamber: [IHK](#ihk), Handwerkskammer, Landwirtschaftskammer, and the
professional chambers of doctors, pharmacists, lawyers, tax advisers, architects and
engineers. **Which one is competent for you decides where your recognition file goes.** See
[recognition-authorities.md](recognition-authorities.md).

### Kenntnisprüfung
An examination used instead of an [Anpassungslehrgang](#anpassungslehrgang) to close
recognition gaps. Faster, harder, and sometimes offered only twice a year.

### Kirchensteuer
Church tax, 8–9% **of your income tax**, payable if you register as a member of a
tax-collecting religious community — including on the **Anmeldung** form in your first week.
See [taxes-and-net-pay.md](taxes-and-net-pay.md).

### Kündigungsfrist
Notice period. Long by international standards, and symmetrical.

### Lehrling
Historical German word for an [Azubi](#auszubildender--azubi). Still heard in the Handwerk,
still standard in Austria, not the legal term.

### Meisterbrief
The master craftsman's qualification. Four parts, including **Teil IV, the AEVO**, which
licenses you to train apprentices. Sits at **DQR level 6 — the same as a Bachelor** — and
grants university entrance without Abitur. See [meister.md](meister.md).

### Meisterpflicht
The requirement to hold a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief) to run a business in an
[Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b) trade.

### Mindestlohn
The statutory minimum wage. Some sectors have a **higher branch minimum** declared under the
**AEntG** — construction, security, building cleaning.

### Probezeit
Probation. One to four months in an [Ausbildung](#ausbildung), usually six in employment.
After it, dismissal protection is strong.

### Rufbereitschaft
On call **from home** — a standby payment plus full pay for hours actually worked.

### Scheinselbständigkeit
Bogus self-employment: working as a contractor while in substance an employee. The client
becomes liable for back social contributions. Relevant to
[software contracting](../jobs/it/softwareentwickler-in.md) and
[Stuhlmiete](../jobs/skilled-trades/friseur-in.md). Enforced by the
[FKS](../jobs/public-service/zollbeamte-r.md).

### Sozialabgaben
Social contributions — pension, unemployment, health, care. Roughly 20–21% of gross,
**capped**, and matched by your employer.

### Steuerklasse
Tax class I–VI, determining monthly withholding rather than total tax owed. The **III/V**
combination distorts Elterngeld and Arbeitslosengeld, which are computed on net pay.

### Tarifvertrag
A collective agreement. Which one applies — TVöD, TV-L, IG Metall, DEHOGA, or none — is
frequently worth 20–30% of the package. See [pay.md](pay.md).

### Unterrichtung
A 40-hour instruction course, notably the basic **§34a** requirement for
[security work](../jobs/security/README.md). **Not a qualification** for visa purposes.

### Verbeamtung
Appointment as a [Beamter](#beamter--beamtin). Gated by citizenship, age and an
**amtsärztliche Untersuchung**.

### Vorbereitungsdienst
The paid preparatory service before full appointment as a [Beamter](#beamter--beamtin) —
the Referendariat for teachers, the police Bachelor, the customs training.

### Westbalkanregelung
**§26(2) BeschV.** Lets nationals of six Western Balkan states take **any** job, qualified or
not, subject to an annual quota. Widely used in hospitality, logistics and construction.

### Zeitzuschläge
Hourly supplements for unsocial hours — night 20%, Sunday 25%, holidays up to 135% under
TVöD, **largely tax-free** under §3b EStG. See
[shift-work-and-supplements.md](shift-work-and-supplements.md).

### Zusatzbeitrag
The supplementary health-insurance contribution set by each individual Krankenkasse, on top
of the 14.6% base rate.
