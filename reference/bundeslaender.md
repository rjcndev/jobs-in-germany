# Choosing a Bundesland

Eight profession files in this repo tell you to "choose the Bundesland carefully" and none
of them helps you do it. This does.

**The short version:** for most professions the state barely matters. For a handful it
decides your training, your pay and whether you can be employed at all — and those are the
ones where the advice is worth acting on.

## Where it genuinely matters

| Profession | What varies | How much |
|---|---|---|
| [**Lehrer/in**](../jobs/education/lehrer-in.md) | The entire system: training structure, subject combinations, **whether you are made a Beamter**, and A13-für-alle status | **Decisive** |
| [**Erzieher/in**](../jobs/education/erzieher-in.md) | **The law itself is state law** — training content, entry requirements and the recognition procedure genuinely differ | **Decisive** |
| [**Polizist/in**](../jobs/public-service/polizist-in.md) | Sixteen separate forces, own recruitment, own Besoldung — and **whether EU nationals may be appointed at all** | **Decisive** |
| [Other Beamte](beamte-vs-angestellte.md) — [fire](../jobs/public-service/feuerwehrmann-frau.md), [tax](../jobs/public-service/steuerbeamte-r.md) | **Besoldung is state law** since 2006, and has diverged | High |
| [Green professions](../jobs/green/README.md) | Whether a **Landwirtschaftskammer exists** at all, or a ministry does the job | High |
| Licensed healthcare | The competent authority differs; **the standard is federal**, so outcomes are broadly consistent | Low — procedural only |
| Everything unregulated | Nothing formally. Only the labour market | Low |

**The distinction to hold on to:** for federally regulated professions the *office* is
state-level but the *standard* is national. For **teaching and Erzieher/in the standard
itself is state law**. That is the difference between paperwork and a different career.

## What else is state law

Things the profession files run into that turn out to be set per Bundesland:

- **Besoldung** — Beamten pay, since the 2006 Föderalismusreform
- **Meisterprämie** — the bonus for passing the [Meisterprüfung](meister.md). Several states
  pay a substantial lump sum; some effectively refund the fees. Others pay nothing
- **Bildungsurlaub / Bildungszeit** — statutory paid educational leave exists in most states
  and not all, with different entitlements
- **Gaststättenrecht** — licensing for [serving alcohol](../jobs/hospitality/barkeeper-in.md),
  and **smoking rules**, which is why some states still have smoking bars
- **Sperrzeiten** — closing hours, set at state and municipal level
- **School systems** — relevant if you are moving with children, and a real factor in
  whether a move between states is disruptive
- **Ladenöffnungszeiten** — shop opening hours, which shape
  [retail and hospitality](../jobs/hospitality/README.md) rosters

## Pay against cost of living

Nominal pay and real pay diverge sharply, and in a direction that is easy to get wrong.

**Private-sector pay** is highest in Bavaria, Baden-Württemberg, Hesse and Hamburg — and so
is housing. Munich, Frankfurt and Stuttgart combine the best salaries in
[engineering](../jobs/engineering/ingenieur-in.md) and
[software](../jobs/it/softwareentwickler-in.md) with the highest rents in the country.

**Public-sector tariffs are national.** TVöD and TV-L pay the same in Chemnitz as in Munich.
That produces a genuine and underused arbitrage: **a [nurse](../jobs/healthcare/pflegefachfrau-pflegefachmann.md),
[MT](../jobs/healthcare/medizinische-technologin-mt-mta.md),
[Erzieher/in](../jobs/education/erzieher-in.md) or
[paramedic](../jobs/healthcare/notfallsanitaeter-in.md) is materially better off in a
low-cost region** than in an expensive one, on identical gross pay.

**Besoldung is not national**, so the same logic does not automatically apply to
[Beamte](beamte-vs-angestellte.md) — compare the specific state's tables against its housing
costs.

Eastern states generally pay less privately, cost considerably less to live in, and have
acute shortages in exactly the [healthcare](../jobs/healthcare/README.md),
[trades](../jobs/skilled-trades/README.md) and [education](../jobs/education/) occupations
this repo covers. For a tariff-paid profession that combination is favourable.

## Practical advice

1. **Work out first whether your profession is one of the decisive ones.** For most of this
   repo, it is not, and you should choose on jobs, housing and where you want to live.
2. **If it is decisive, research the specific state before filing anything.** Recognition
   procedures for teaching and Erzieher/in are not portable, and restarting in another state
   costs months.
3. **Check the Meisterprämie** before paying for a [Meisterprüfung](meister.md).
4. **If you are tariff-paid, take cost of living seriously.** It is the largest lever on your
   real income that you actually control.
5. **Moving states later is possible in almost every case** — but for teaching, policing and
   Erzieher/in it is a negotiation, not a transfer.
