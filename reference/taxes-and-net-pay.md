# Tax and what actually reaches your account

Every figure in this repo is **gross**. This explains what comes off it, and why comparing
gross salaries across professions — including in [pay.md](pay.md) — overstates the
differences between them.

As a rough orientation: **net is commonly around 60–65% of gross** for an employee at the
salary levels in this repo, and **the percentage falls as gross rises**. A job paying twice
another does not pay twice as much into your account.

## Two separate deductions, often muddled

| | **Steuern** | **Sozialabgaben** |
|---|---|---|
| What | Income tax, church tax, Soli | Pension, unemployment, health, care |
| Rate | **Progressive** | **Flat percentages, capped** |
| Capped? | No | **Yes** — above the ceilings you pay no more |
| Who else pays | — | Your employer pays roughly the same again |

Because social contributions are **capped** and tax is **progressive**, the two pull in
opposite directions as income rises.

## Social contributions

Employee share, split roughly equally with your employer:

| Contribution | Employee share, approximately |
|---|---|
| **Rentenversicherung** (pension) | ~9.3% |
| **Arbeitslosenversicherung** (unemployment) | ~1.3% |
| **Krankenversicherung** (health) | ~7.3% + half the Zusatzbeitrag — see [health insurance](health-insurance.md) |
| **Pflegeversicherung** (long-term care) | ~1.8%, **more if you have no children**, less with several |

Roughly **20–21% of gross** in total, up to annually re-set ceilings. Those ceilings move
every January; look them up rather than trusting a figure written here.

**[Beamte](beamte-vs-angestellte.md) pay none of the pension or unemployment contributions
and only a small private health premium against Beihilfe.** That is the entire reason their
gross is not comparable to an employee's.

## Income tax

- A **Grundfreibetrag** — a tax-free allowance, indexed annually
- Then a **progressive** rate rising from 14% to the **Spitzensteuersatz of 42%**, with a
  45% band at the top
- **Solidaritätszuschlag** — abolished for roughly the bottom 90% of taxpayers since 2021,
  still payable by high earners
- **Kirchensteuer** — 8–9% **of your income tax**, payable only if you are a registered
  member of a tax-collecting religious community

### Kirchensteuer is a trap for new arrivals

When you register your address (**Anmeldung**), the form asks your religion. **Stating one
that collects church tax enrols you**, and the deduction appears on every payslip
thereafter. Leaving later requires a formal **Kirchenaustritt** at a registry office or
court, with a fee, and it takes effect only from that point.

It is not a trick — Germans expect the question — but people fill in the form on day three
in the country, in German, and are surprised months later. Answer it deliberately.

## Steuerklassen

Your tax class determines **how much is withheld each month**, not how much tax you
ultimately owe. Differences are settled in the annual assessment.

| Klasse | For |
|---|---|
| **I** | Single, divorced, widowed |
| **II** | Single parent, with an additional allowance |
| **III** | Married, where the partner takes V — **low withholding** |
| **IV** | Married, both similar incomes |
| **IV mit Faktor** | Married, withholding matched to each partner's actual share |
| **V** | The partner of a III — **high withholding** |
| **VI** | A second and further jobs |

**The III/V combination is the one that causes arguments.** The higher earner takes III and
sees a large net; the lower earner takes V and sees a startlingly small one. Nothing is
actually lost — it evens out at assessment — but it distorts income-linked benefits such as
Elterngeld and Arbeitslosengeld, which are calculated on *net* pay. **IV mit Faktor** gives
the same annual result with a fairer monthly split.

**A reform replacing III/V with IV mit Faktor has been announced repeatedly and repeatedly
delayed.** Check its current status.

## Ehegattensplitting

Married couples and registered partners may be assessed jointly, with the tax computed as
though each earned half the combined income. Because the rate is progressive, this is worth
a great deal when incomes are **unequal** and almost nothing when they are similar.

It interacts with the [free family health insurance](health-insurance.md) in GKV: for a
single-income household, the German system is markedly more generous than the gross figures
suggest.

## What you can deduct

Employees get a lump-sum **Werbungskostenpauschale** automatically. Exceeding it is worth
claiming:

- **Pendlerpauschale** — a per-kilometre commuting allowance
- **Homeoffice-Pauschale** — a per-day allowance for working from home
- **Arbeitsmittel** — tools, work clothing, professional literature, a computer.
  Directly relevant to the trades, where tools are bought personally
- **Fortbildungskosten** — courses and exams, including the
  [Meisterprüfung](meister.md) and the
  [Steuerberater exam](../jobs/commercial/steuerfachangestellte-r.md)
- **Doppelte Haushaltsführung** — maintaining a second home for work, relevant to
  [Montage](../jobs/industrial/industriemechaniker-in.md) and commissioning roles
- **Umzugskosten** — relocation for work

## What is not taxed

Several things this repo treats as real income are **tax-free**, which is why net
comparisons differ from gross ones:

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Night, Sunday and holiday supplements** | **§3b EStG**, within limits — see [shift work and supplements](shift-work-and-supplements.md) |
| **Tips** | **§3 Nr. 51 EStG**, without limit — [hospitality](../jobs/hospitality/README.md), [hairdressing](../jobs/skilled-trades/friseur-in.md) |
| **Spesen** — per-diems for time away | Within statutory rates — [road haulage](../jobs/logistics/berufskraftfahrer-in.md), Montage |

A shift-working nurse and a nine-to-five office worker on identical gross do **not** take
home the same amount.

## The annual return

Not compulsory for every employee, but usually worth filing — refunds are common,
particularly in a year with a move, a course, or a change of job. **ELSTER** is the official
free portal; commercial software and Lohnsteuerhilfevereine are the usual alternatives.
Deadlines differ depending on whether you file yourself or through an adviser.

**Run your own figures through a Brutto-Netto-Rechner** with your Steuerklasse, church-tax
status, children and Bundesland before comparing two offers. It is the only way the gross
figures in this repo become comparable to each other.
