# Konstruktionsmechaniker/in (Structural Metalworker / Fabricator)

> Builds structures out of steel: plate, profile and tube, cut, formed, welded and erected.
> The trade where **welding certification, not the job title, decides what you may work on.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official title** | Konstruktionsmechaniker/in |
| **Protected title** | **No** |
| **Category** | industrial |
| **Typical qualification** | Duale Ausbildung, 3.5 years, **IHK** exam |
| **Regulated** | No — but welding and EN 1090 gate the work |
| **Last reviewed** | 2026-09 |

## Its Handwerk twin: Metallbauer/in

The same work exists in both systems, and the difference is the one this category is built
around:

| | **Konstruktionsmechaniker/in** | **Metallbauer/in** |
|---|---|---|
| System | Industrie, **IHK** | Handwerk, **HWK** |
| Anlage A | — | **Yes — Meisterpflicht** |
| Self-employment | **No right to open a business** | Meisterbrief opens one |
| Typical employer | Stahlbau firms, shipyards, plant builders | Metal workshops, Schlossereien |
| Tariff | IG Metall / sector agreements | Handwerk agreements, generally lower |

Exactly the pattern of
[Elektroniker Betriebstechnik vs the Handwerk electrician](elektroniker-in-betriebstechnik.md):
comparable work, better industrial pay, no business rights. Metallbauer has its own
Fachrichtungen (Konstruktionstechnik, Metallgestaltung, Nutzfahrzeugbau).

## Fachrichtungen

| Fachrichtung | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Schweißtechnik** | Welded fabrication — the broadest route |
| **Feinblechbautechnik** | Sheet metal: enclosures, ducting, housings |
| **Schiffbau** | Ship sections and hull structures |
| **Ausrüstungstechnik** | Piping, plant and equipment fit-out |

## Welding is the qualification that matters

The Ausbildung gets you hired. **Welder qualification certificates to DIN EN ISO 9606 decide
what you may actually weld** — separately per process (MAG, TIG, MMA), material group,
thickness and position. They are examined, time-limited, and require periodic confirmation
to stay valid.

Above them sits the **DVS ladder**: Schweißfachmann, Schweißtechniker, Schweißfachingenieur.
These are well paid and in demand, because **EN 1090** requires certified welding
coordination for structural steel that carries CE marking — the firm cannot fabricate
load-bearing structure without it.

This is the repo's sixth instance of the qualification-gates-the-work pattern, after
[EFK](elektroniker-in-betriebstechnik.md),
[BaFin registration](../commercial/bankkaufmann-frau.md), §34a security, driving licences and
[Hochvolt](../skilled-trades/kraftfahrzeugmechatroniker-in.md) — and the most granular:
here the gate is per weld type, not per person.

## What the job involves

Reading structural drawings; cutting and forming plate, profile and tube; jig setup;
welding; assembly and dimensional checking; and frequently **Montage** — erecting or
installing the structure on site, often at height and outdoors.

Sectors: **Stahlbau** (structural steel, bridges, halls), **Behälter- und Apparatebau**
(tanks and pressure vessels), shipbuilding, plant construction, and **offshore wind** —
towers, transition pieces and foundations, which is the clearest growth area and another
Energiewende link alongside [SHK](../skilled-trades/anlagenmechaniker-in-shk.md) and the
electrical trades.

The work is physically heavy, frequently outdoors, and involves noise, fumes and working at
height. Occupational health is a genuine long-term consideration, not a formality.

## Qualification route

- **3.5 years dual**, industrial employer plus Berufsschule, **IHK** exam
- **Entry:** formally open; Hauptschul- or mittlerer Schulabschluss typical
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €1,100–1,400/month gross at tariff employers
- **Ladder:** welding qualifications first, then **Schweißfachmann/Schweißtechniker (DVS)**,
  **Industriemeister Metall**, **Techniker**, or quality and inspection roles

## Pay

**Tariff-bound where IG Metall or a sector agreement applies. Market estimates** — see the
[category README](README.md#pay-is-better-here-and-not-verifiable):

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Newly qualified (tariff-bound) | €3,200 – €3,800 |
| Experienced | €3,800 – €4,600 |
| With sought-after welding certificates | €4,200 – €5,000 |
| Schweißfachmann / Schweißtechniker | €4,500 – €5,500 |
| Industriemeister / Techniker | €4,700 – €5,800 |

**Montage is the earnings lever.** Site work adds tax-free per-diems and travel allowances
that can lift take-home well above the table, at the cost of long periods away —
the same trade-off as commissioning in [Mechatronik](mechatroniker-in.md).

Stahlbau firms outside the metal and electrical agreements pay noticeably less; ask which
agreement applies.

## Demand and outlook

Solid overall, with two opposite currents worth separating:

- **Growing:** offshore wind, grid and energy infrastructure, plant construction, defence.
- **Shrunk:** German civil shipbuilding is a fraction of its former size. What remains is
  concentrated in cruise construction — itself financially turbulent in recent years — and
  in naval and submarine work, which is comparatively secure.

If you take the Schiffbau Fachrichtung, understand that you are choosing a regionally
concentrated and politically exposed sector, not a general one.

## Foreign candidates

1. **To work:** nothing required. Unregulated.
2. **For the visa:** **IHK FOSA** equivalence assessment.
3. **Welding certificates do not transfer.** Expect to sit the German/EN examinations for
   each process and position you need. Budget time for this — it is the single biggest
   practical obstacle, and more consequential here than in any other trade in this repo.
4. **Language: B1–B2.** Drawings, welding procedure specifications (WPS), safety briefings
   and site coordination are in German; site safety in particular is not a place for
   guesswork.

## Pitfalls

- **Certificates, not experience, decide what you may weld.** Years of skilled welding
  abroad grant nothing until re-examined.
- **No business rights** from this qualification — that is the Handwerk Metallbauer route.
- **Montage pay comes with sustained time away.** Ask for the real annual figure.
- **Fachrichtung matters regionally** — Schiffbau especially ties you to the coast.
- **Physical wear** is real. Plan a route toward Schweißfachmann, inspection or Meister.
