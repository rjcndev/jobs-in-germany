# Healthcare

The most heavily regulated sector in this repo. **Almost** every profession here needs a
**state licence** before you may work at all — recognition is not optional, and the pattern
is near-identical across them, so it is collected once here.

**The two exceptions are worth knowing first.** The assistant occupations —
[MFA](medizinische-fachangestellte-r.md), and the dental, veterinary and pharmacy
equivalents — are **not regulated**; they are ordinary dual Ausbildungen, examined by the
chamber of the profession they assist. And
[Pflegefachassistenz](pflegefachassistenz-pflegehelfer-in.md) is regulated by the
**Bundesländer** rather than federally, in sixteen different versions.

## The recognition path, in general

1. **Apply to the competent authority of the Bundesland** where you intend to work —
   Landesprüfungsamt, Bezirksregierung, or Landesamt für Gesundheit, depending on the state.
   **There is no national office.** Start at
   [anerkennung-in-deutschland.de](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de).
2. **EU/EEA/Switzerland:** doctors, dentists, nurses, midwives, vets and pharmacists have
   **automatic recognition** under Directive 2005/36/EC. This is much the shorter path.
   **It does not extend to the rest of this folder** — physiotherapy, psychotherapy,
   the therapy professions, PTA, MT/MTA and ATA/OTA are all assessed individually even
   from inside the EU. See [Architekt](../engineering/architekt-in.md) for the full list
   of seven and why it is closed.
3. **Third countries:** a **Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung** compares your training to the German
   curriculum. Gaps close through either an **Anpassungslehrgang** (supervised practice,
   typically 6–12 months) or a **Kenntnisprüfung** (examination). The course is safer, the
   exam faster.
4. **Language:** **B2** is the common statutory minimum. Doctors, dentists and pharmacists
   additionally sit a **Fachsprachprüfung** at their Kammer — a profession-specific oral
   exam that is the usual failure point, not clinical knowledge. What that exam actually
   consists of, and which certificate each authority accepts, is in
   [language certificates](../../reference/language-certificates.md).
5. **Visa:** **§16d AufenthG** to enter while recognition runs, then the skilled-worker
   permit. Nursing additionally has the **Anerkennungspartnerschaft** (§16d(3)), which
   allows entry at A2 German.

## Working in the interim

Most of these professions have a lower tier you can legally work in while recognition runs —
[Pflegehelfer/in](pflegefachassistenz-pflegehelfer-in.md) for nursing, Rettungssanitäter for
paramedics, Berufserlaubnis for doctors and pharmacists. **Agree in writing what the contract
converts to on licence, and at what pay grade.** Drifting in the interim role is the single
most common bad outcome, and the nursing-assistant file sets out in detail how to stop it
happening to you.

## Pay

Public-sector healthcare pay is **tariff-verified** throughout this repo — TVöD-K, TVöD-P,
TVöD SuE, TV-Ärzte/VKA and TVöD EG N all have published scales. See
[reference/pay.md](../../reference/pay.md#reliability-of-these-figures) for the tables and
their expiry dates. Church providers (Caritas, Diakonie) use comparable AVR agreements;
private operators often pay less.
