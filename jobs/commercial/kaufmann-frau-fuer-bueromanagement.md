# Kaufmann/-frau für Büromanagement (Office Management Clerk)

> Germany's single most common Ausbildung. Administration, correspondence, scheduling,
> invoicing and order processing — in essentially every sector.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official title** | Kaufmann/-frau für Büromanagement, since 2014 |
| **Former titles** | Bürokaufmann/-frau, Kaufmann/-frau für Bürokommunikation, Fachangestellte/r für Bürokommunikation |
| **Protected title** | **No** |
| **Category** | commercial |
| **Typical qualification** | Duale Ausbildung, 3 years, IHK exam |
| **Regulated** | No |
| **Last reviewed** | 2026-09 |

## What the job involves

Correspondence and document management, appointment and travel coordination, invoice
processing and order handling, basic bookkeeping, purchasing support, and customer contact.
The content varies enormously with the employer — a Steuerkanzlei, a machine-tool
manufacturer and a city council give the same job title completely different substance.

## Qualification route

- **3 years dual**, employer plus Berufsschule. The occupation was created in 2014 by
  merging three older clerical qualifications.
- **Two Wahlqualifikationen** chosen from a list of ten — assistance and secretariat,
  purchasing and logistics, marketing and sales, HR, accounting, public finance, and
  others. **This choice shapes your career more than the base qualification does.** Pick
  toward a specialism with a pay ceiling.
- **Entry:** mittlerer Schulabschluss typical; formally open
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €950–1,200/month gross, higher in public sector and
  tariff-bound industry
- **Gestreckte Abschlussprüfung** — a two-part IHK exam spread across the training

## Pay

| Setting | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Newly qualified, private sector | €2,500 – €3,000 |
| Experienced | €3,000 – €3,700 |
| Public sector (TVöD EG 5–6) | €3,000 – €3,700 |
| Tariff-bound industry (IG Metall etc.) | €3,300 – €4,200 |

The spread here is driven almost entirely by **who employs you**, not by how good you are.
Tariff-bound industrial employers and the public sector pay well above small private firms
for identical work, with better hours and security. This is the single most useful thing to
know about the occupation.

## A candid note on prospects

Routine clerical work is the part of the German labour market most exposed to automation,
and the volume of pure administrative roles has been shrinking for years. The
qualification remains valuable as a **foundation** — it feeds naturally into
Fachwirt qualifications, HR, accounting, controlling or purchasing specialisms — but
staying in generalist office administration for a whole career is a weaker bet than it was.
Choose the Wahlqualifikationen accordingly.

## Foreign candidates

1. **To work:** nothing required. Unregulated.
2. **For the skilled-worker visa:** you need a **Gleichwertigkeitsfeststellung** via
   **IHK FOSA**. Foreign commercial qualifications are often found only partly equivalent
   because the German dual system's practical component has no counterpart in purely
   academic business qualifications.
3. **No EU Blue Card** — salary is below the threshold.
4. **Language: B2 minimum, realistically C1.** This is a German-language job in a way that
   IT is not — correspondence, phone contact, and legal-administrative vocabulary. The
   language bar here is higher than in most trades despite the lower pay.

## Pitfalls

- **The employer determines the pay**, and candidates routinely under-target. Compare a
  tariff-bound offer against a non-tariff one before assuming they are similar.
- **Wahlqualifikationen are chosen early and read later.** Do not let the employer pick
  them by default.
- **Recognition gaps** for foreign business degrees are common — a BBA is not automatically
  equivalent to a dual commercial qualification in German eyes.
