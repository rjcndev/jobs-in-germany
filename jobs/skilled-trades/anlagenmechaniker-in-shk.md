# Anlagenmechaniker/in SHK (Plumbing, Heating & AC Technician)

> Sanitär-, Heizungs- und Klimatechnik. Installs and services heating, plumbing and
> ventilation — and is currently at the centre of Germany's heating transition.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official title** | Anlagenmechaniker/in für Sanitär-, Heizungs- und Klimatechnik |
| **Protected title** | Employment: no. Self-employment: yes — Handwerksordnung Anlage A |
| **Category** | skilled-trades |
| **Typical qualification** | Duale Ausbildung, 3.5 years, Gesellenprüfung |
| **Regulated** | Meisterpflicht for self-employment |
| **Last reviewed** | 2026-09 |

## What the job involves

Installing and servicing heating systems, bathrooms, water and gas pipework, and
ventilation. The mix has shifted decisively: **heat pumps, solar thermal, hydraulic
balancing and hybrid heating systems** now dominate new work, driven by the
**Gebäudeenergiegesetz (GEG)** and its renewable-heating requirements.

Customer-facing, on-site, physically demanding, and heavily norm-governed (DIN, DVGW for
gas and water).

## Qualification route

- **3.5 years dual**, employer plus Berufsschule
- **Entry:** formally open; Hauptschulabschluss typical
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €900–1,300/month gross, rising by year
- **Ends with** the **Gesellenprüfung**
- **Meisterprüfung** afterwards for business ownership — same Aufstiegs-BAföG and
  state Meisterprämie support as other Anlage A trades

**Gas work additionally requires DVGW certification** for the business; this is a company
qualification, not just a personal one.

## Pay

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Geselle, newly qualified | €2,700 – €3,200 |
| Geselle, experienced | €3,200 – €3,900 |
| Meister (employed) | €3,900 – €4,900 |
| Meister with own business | Very variable; strong in the current market |

Overtime and emergency callouts (Notdienst) add meaningfully. Southern Germany pays highest.

## Demand

Severe shortage, and politically salient. The heating transition requires far more heat-pump
capable installers than exist, and the trade's demographics are poor — a large share of
current Meister are near retirement, which makes **Betriebsübernahme** (taking over an
existing business) a realistic path for an ambitious Meister.

## Foreign-trained candidates

1. **To be employed:** no recognition legally required; a Handwerkskammer
   **Gleichwertigkeitsfeststellung** still helps with grading and visa.
2. **To be self-employed:** Handwerksrolle entry, requiring the Meisterbrief, an equivalence
   decision, an **Ausnahmebewilligung (§8 HwO)**, or an employed Betriebsleiter. EU/EEA
   nationals have the easier §9 HwO route.
3. **Language:** **B1–B2** minimum. This trade involves constant customer contact in
   people's homes — language is a bigger practical barrier here than the technical work.
4. **Visa:** skilled-worker route, or §16a for an Ausbildung. On shortage lists.

## Pitfalls

- **Heat-pump competence is the dividing line.** Installers whose experience is gas-boiler
  only are finding the market move away from them. Training exists — take it.
- **DVGW and gas certification** is a business-level requirement that surprises people
  planning self-employment.
- **Physical wear** is real; the trade has high rates of early occupational disability.
  Plan a route toward Meister, planning or sales.
- **Customer-facing German** matters more than in most trades.
