# Services and other sectors

The occupations here belong to none of the chamber-defined sectors that organise the rest of
this repo. Some are Handwerk, some IHK, some have **no qualification at all** — and that
last group is the reason this folder exists.

## Files with no qualification to write about

Most files in this repo are built around a qualification: what it is, how long it takes, who
examines it, and how a foreign equivalent is assessed. Two of the files here —
[Reinigungskraft](reinigungskraft.md) and [Haushaltshilfe](haushaltshilfe.md) — have none of
that, because **the work requires no qualification and therefore has no recognition and no
equivalence step.**

Leaving them out would have been the easy choice, and the wrong one: between them they are
one of the most common first jobs for people arriving in Germany. So the frame changes.
Instead of qualification and recognition, those files are about **employment status, minimum
wage and enforcement** — which is what actually determines whether the work is decent or not.

## Cleaning is three situations, not one

| Tier | What it is |
|---|---|
| **[Gebäudereiniger/in](gebaeudereiniger-in.md)** | The skilled trade. Three-year Ausbildung, HWK examination, **Anlage B1** — so no Meisterpflicht. A real profession with a ladder to Objektleitung and Meister |
| **[Reinigungskraft](reinigungskraft.md)** | **No qualification.** Employed by cleaning contractors, paid the AEntG sector minimum, frequently part-time, Minijob or agency. Most of the sector by headcount |
| **[Haushaltshilfe](haushaltshilfe.md)** | Private households. The narrowest case and the one with the real legal story: **the overwhelming majority of it is undeclared** |

Conflating them — as English usually does, with one word — hides the fact that they sit under
different law, different pay floors and different enforcement.

## Common threads

- **[Minijob and geringfügige Beschäftigung](../../reference/minijob-und-geringfuegige-beschaeftigung.md)**
  is the background law for most of this folder, and it is where the pension opt-out trap
  and the Haushaltsscheck procedure are explained.
- **[Employment basics](../../reference/employment-basics.md)** matters more here than
  anywhere else in the repo, because these are the jobs where statutory rights are most often
  simply not given.
- **Enforcement** in several of these sectors is by the **Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit**,
  the customs unit staffed by the [Zollbeamte](../public-service/zollbeamte-r.md) profiled
  elsewhere here.
