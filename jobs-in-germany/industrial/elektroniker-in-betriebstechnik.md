## Not the same trade as the Handwerk Elektroniker {#not-the-same-trade-as-the-handwerk-elektroniker}

Two parallel systems train electricians:

- **Handwerk** — Elektroniker/in with a Fachrichtung (Energie- und Gebäudetechnik,
  Automatisierungs- und Systemtechnik, Informations- und Telekommunikationstechnik, and
  since 2021 Gebäudesystemintegration). Handwerkskammer, Anlage A, Meister for
  self-employment.
- **Industrie** — Elektroniker/in für Betriebstechnik and its siblings (Automatisierungs-
  technik, Geräte und Systeme, Maschinen und Antriebstechnik, Gebäude- und
  Infrastruktursysteme). **IHK**, industrial employers, IG Metall.

See the [category README](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/) for what follows from that.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Installing, commissioning and maintaining the electrical side of production plant:
switchgear (Schaltanlagen), power distribution, drives, motors, sensors and actuators,
and **PLC (SPS)** fault-finding. The core of the role is **Instandhaltung** — keeping
lines running and diagnosing faults fast, because downtime is the thing the plant is
actually paying you to prevent.

Production runs around the clock, so shift work and **Rufbereitschaft** are normal and are
a significant part of take-home.

**Elektrofachkraft (EFK):** under **DGUV Vorschrift 3**, only a qualified Elektrofachkraft
may work independently on electrical installations; an *elektrotechnisch unterwiesene
Person* (EuP) may do only narrowly defined tasks under instruction. Your Ausbildung makes
you an EFK. This is a safety-law status attached to the person, not to the job title — and
it, not the title, is what gates the work.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3.5 years dual**, industrial employer plus Berufsschule, **IHK** exam
- **Entry:** mittlerer Schulabschluss typical; formally open
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €1,150–1,400/month gross at IG Metall employers, among the
  better-paid trainings
- **Ladder:** **Industriemeister Elektrotechnik**, **staatlich geprüfte/r Techniker/in
  (Elektrotechnik)**, or a part-time Bachelor. Large employers routinely part-fund these.

## Pay {#pay}

**IG Metall / IG BCE tariff, ERA-graded. Market estimates — see the
[category README](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/#pay-is-better-here-and-not-verifiable) for why these cannot be
verified against a single scale.** Approximate gross monthly, 2026:

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Newly qualified (tariff-bound) | €3,300 – €3,900 |
| Experienced | €3,900 – €4,700 |
| Industriemeister / Techniker | €4,700 – €5,800 |
| Instandhaltungsleitung | €5,500 – €6,800 |

Plus a **13th-month payment**, holiday pay, a **35-hour week** in the metal and electrical
industries, and shift and on-call supplements on top. Compare the total package against the
[Handwerk electrician](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/elektroniker-in-energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/)'s
€2,800–€4,000 rather than comparing base salaries.

Non-tariff industrial employers pay considerably less. Ask which agreement applies.

## Demand {#demand}

High and broadening. Automation, semiconductor and battery plants, grid and substation work,
and the general shortage of maintenance electricians all pull in the same direction.
Instandhaltung is also comparatively automation-resistant: it is the function that fixes the
automation.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work:** nothing required. The occupation is unregulated.
2. **For the visa:** **IHK FOSA** equivalence assessment of a foreign qualification.
3. **EFK status** is a separate matter from recognition: the employer must satisfy itself
   you meet DGUV V3 requirements before letting you work unsupervised. Foreign training
   often does not map to German standards, and a top-up assessment is common.
4. **Language: B1–B2.** Safety instruction, VDE norms, documentation and shift handover are
   all in German.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **This qualification does not let you open an electrical business.** That needs
  Handwerksrolle entry for the Anlage A trade, normally via the
  [Handwerk route](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/elektroniker-in-energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/) and a
  Meisterbrief. People discover this after registering a Gewerbe.
- **Tariff vs. non-tariff** changes the package by 20–30%, not by a few percent.
- **VDE norms** are the standard your work is judged against, whatever your experience
  elsewhere.
- **Shift and on-call** are a large share of pay — and of the job's cost to you. Read the
  Schichtplan before signing.
