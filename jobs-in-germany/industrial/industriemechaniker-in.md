## Einsatzgebiete, not Fachrichtungen {#einsatzgebiete-not-fachrichtungen}

The 2004 reform of the metal occupations collapsed several older trades into one
qualification. Instead of certified specialisations it has **Einsatzgebiete** — typically
Instandhaltung, Maschinen- und Anlagenbau, Produktionstechnik, or Feingerätebau.

The distinction matters: an **Einsatzgebiet is defined by your employer**, not awarded by
the chamber, and it does not appear on your certificate as a restriction. That makes the
qualification broader and more mobile than a Handwerk Fachrichtung — but it also means
**what you actually learned depends heavily on where you trained.** Ask a prospective
employer what their trainees are exposed to, and expect to be asked the same in reverse.

## Confusable titles {#confusable-titles}

German metalworking splits into several distinct occupations that outsiders read as one:

| Occupation | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **Industriemechaniker/in** | This one — assembly, machines, plant, maintenance |
| [Mechatroniker/in](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/mechatroniker-in/) | Adds electrical and control systems to the mechanical side |
| **Zerspanungsmechaniker/in** | CNC machining specialist — turning and milling to tolerance |
| **Konstruktionsmechaniker/in** | Steel and metal structures, welding-heavy |
| **Werkzeugmechaniker/in** | Tools, dies and moulds |
| **Anlagenmechaniker/in** (industrial) | Piping and process plant — **not** the Handwerk [SHK trade](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/anlagenmechaniker-in-shk/) of almost the same name |

That last row is a genuine trap. **Anlagenmechaniker/in** and **Anlagenmechaniker/in für
Sanitär-, Heizungs- und Klimatechnik** are different occupations under different chambers —
industrial IHK plant work versus Handwerk building services.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Manufacturing and fitting components; assembling machines and production lines
(**Montage**); commissioning; and **Instandhaltung** — inspection, maintenance and repair to
keep plant running. Practical work spans conventional machining, filing and fitting,
pneumatics and hydraulics, basic welding, measurement and quality checking, and reading
technical drawings.

Maintenance roles run shifts and on-call where production is continuous, which adds
meaningfully to pay.

**Welding certification** (Schweißerprüfung to DIN EN ISO 9606) is a separate, renewable
qualification that gates specific work and is normally employer-funded — the same
short-ticket pattern as the Staplerschein or Hochvolt levels elsewhere in this repo.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3.5 years dual**, industrial employer plus Berufsschule, **IHK** exam
- **Entry:** mittlerer Schulabschluss typical; formally open
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €1,150–1,400/month gross at IG Metall employers
- **Ladder:** **Industriemeister Metall**, **staatlich geprüfte/r Techniker/in
  (Maschinenbau)**, Schweißfachmann, REFA, or a part-time Bachelor. Large employers
  routinely part-fund these.

## Pay {#pay}

**IG Metall / IG BCE tariff, ERA-graded. Market estimates** — see the
[category README](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/#pay-is-better-here-and-not-verifiable). Approximate gross
monthly, 2026:

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Newly qualified (tariff-bound) | €3,300 – €3,900 |
| Experienced | €3,900 – €4,700 |
| Industriemeister / Techniker | €4,700 – €5,800 |
| Instandhaltungs- or Montageleitung | €5,500 – €6,800 |

Plus the 13th month, holiday pay and a 35-hour week at tariff employers, and shift and
on-call supplements on top. **Montage abroad** — installing and commissioning plant at
customer sites — adds substantial tax-free per-diems, the same earnings lever as
commissioning work in [Mechatronik](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/mechatroniker-in/).

Non-tariff employers, and small machine builders in particular, pay considerably less.

## Demand and outlook — with a caveat {#demand-and-outlook--with-a-caveat}

Demand is solid, and **Instandhaltung is the durable end of it**: maintaining plant is
comparatively automation-resistant, because it is the function that repairs the automation.

The caveat is the same one the [Industriekaufmann](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/industriekaufmann-frau/)
file carries. German industry, automotive above all, is in a structural transition; electric
drivetrains use far fewer machined parts than combustion ones, and supplier consolidation is
real. Skills in maintenance, plant construction and process industries transfer well. A
career anchored to one combustion-era supplier does not.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work:** nothing required. Unregulated.
2. **For the visa:** **IHK FOSA** equivalence assessment.
3. **Welding certificates** do not transfer automatically — expect to sit the German
   examination for the processes and positions you need.
4. **Language: B1–B2.** Drawings, work instructions, safety briefings and shift handover are
   in German; Montage teams abroad often operate in English.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Your Einsatzgebiet is your employer's choice.** Two people with the same certificate can
  have very different practical experience — check what a training place actually covers.
- **Confusable titles**, above. Particularly Anlagenmechaniker industrial vs. SHK.
- **Tariff vs. non-tariff** is worth 20–30% of the package.
- **Montage travel** pays well and costs a great deal of time away. Ask for the real number
  of nights per year.
- **Sector concentration risk** — diversify toward maintenance or process industries rather
  than a single automotive supplier.
