## The five Fachrichtungen {#the-five-fachrichtungen}


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Fachrichtung | What it is | Outlook |
|---|---|---|
| **Rinderhaltung** | Dairy and beef. Herd management, milking parlours and robots, breeding, feed | **Strong demand for qualified people**, especially herd managers |
| **Schweinehaltung** | Pig production | **Under severe structural pressure** — see below |
| **Geflügelhaltung** | Laying hens, broilers, turkeys. Large, integrated, technical | Stable |
| **Schäferei** | Shepherding, including transhumant **Wanderschäferei** | Tiny, subsidised, culturally protected |
| **Imkerei** | Beekeeping — honey, pollination services, queen breeding | Very small as a full-time occupation |

</div>


**Rinderhaltung is the mainstream choice**, and the one with a genuine employed career: large
dairy units, particularly in eastern Germany, employ herd managers, milking specialists and
calf-rearing staff on defined roles and shifts. It is the part of the green sector most like
ordinary skilled employment.

## Pig farming — the honest picture {#pig-farming--the-honest-picture}

German pig production has contracted sharply. Herd numbers and holdings have fallen year on
year, driven by:

- **Tierwohl requirements** — higher space and enrichment standards, the phase-out of
  conventional farrowing crates, and the **Haltungsform** labelling that has shifted retail
  demand. Conversion costs are large and often not financeable
- **African swine fever**, which has restricted exports and imposed biosecurity costs
- **Falling domestic pork consumption**
- **Succession failure** at exactly the moment large investment is required

What follows for a worker: **fewer, larger, more technical units**, employing qualified staff
rather than family labour. Qualified stockpeople are scarce, and the remaining businesses pay
for them. But this is a shrinking sector, and the repo's convention is to say so.

## Schäferei — the edge case <a id="schäferei--the-edge-case"></a>

A few hundred people in Germany herd sheep full time. The occupation survives because it is
**paid to exist**: the grazing it provides maintains dykes, heathland, chalk grassland and
orchards that have no other economic use, and conservation and agri-environment payments —
including a headage premium for grazing livestock — are a large share of income.

- **Wanderschäferei** — moving flocks along traditional routes — is recognised as
  **intangible cultural heritage**, which is a fair indication of how endangered it is
- **Incomes are among the lowest in this repo**, hours are long, and the work is solitary
- **Wolves** have made herd protection a permanent cost and a permanent political argument.
  Fencing and livestock guardian dogs are subsidised, and they are also work
- Entry is realistic through the Ausbildung, and the shortage of qualified shepherds is acute

If you want it, it is available. Nobody should enter it expecting to be paid what the
responsibility is worth.

## What the work involves, across the Fachrichtungen {#what-the-work-involves-across-the-fachrichtungen}

- **Feeding, health monitoring and breeding**, increasingly with sensor and herd-management
  software
- **Medicine records and antibiotic reporting**, which are legally mandated and audited
- **Animal welfare compliance** under the Tierschutz-Nutztierhaltungsverordnung
- **Hygiene and biosecurity**, which is not optional in pig and poultry production
- **Milking, lambing, farrowing, hatching** — the parts that do not respect working hours
- **Machinery and building services** — ventilation, feeding systems, slurry handling

Livestock work is **every day of the year**. Weekend and holiday rosters are part of the job
in any unit large enough to have staff.

## Qualification and above {#qualification-and-above}

- **3 years dual**, examined by the **Landwirtschaftskammer** or state authority
- **Above it:** **Tierwirtschaftsmeister/in** in the Fachrichtung, **Agrartechniker**, or a
  Bachelor in Agrarwissenschaften leading to breeding, advisory, feed-industry and veterinary
  support roles
- **Adjacent qualifications** worth knowing: **Klauenpfleger** (hoof trimming) and
  **Besamungstechniker** (AI technician) are short, well-paid specialisms

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work: no recognition required.**
2. **For the visa: yes** — **Landwirtschaftskammer** or state authority, not IHK FOSA.
3. **Language: B1–B2.** Animal health, medicine records and machinery safety.
4. **Visa:** §18a, **§16a**, or **§26(2) BeschV**.
5. **Livestock experience transfers well in substance** — the animals are the same — but
   German documentation, medicine and welfare law is where equivalence assessments find the
   gap.
6. **Eastern Germany is where the employed jobs are**, as in [Landwirt/in](/jobs-in-germany/green/landwirt-in/).

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Choose the Fachrichtung with its outlook in mind.** Rinderhaltung and Schweinehaltung
  point in opposite directions right now.
- **Livestock does not take holidays.** Establish the roster before accepting.
- **Zoonoses and dust** are the real occupational health issues — pig and poultry houses in
  particular, with respiratory protection obligations.
- **Schäferei is a vocation with a subsidy attached**, not a career with an income.
- **Herd-management software and sensor systems** are now the differentiator for pay. Learn
  them.
