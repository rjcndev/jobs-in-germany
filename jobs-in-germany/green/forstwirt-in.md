## Three different jobs English calls "forester" {#three-different-jobs-english-calls-forester}

This is the most common confusion about German forestry, and the three are separated by
years of training and, frequently, by employment status:


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Role | Qualification | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Forstwirt/in** | 3-year Ausbildung | The manual trade — this file |
| **Forstwirtschaftsmeister/in** | Meister, on top of the Ausbildung | Crew and operations leadership |
| **Förster/in — Revierleiter/in** | **Bachelor** in Forstwirtschaft + Anwärterdienst, gehobener Forstdienst | Manages a district. **Frequently verbeamtet** |
| *(höherer Forstdienst)* | Master + Referendariat | Senior forest administration |

</div>


A Forstwirt does not become a Förster by experience. It requires the degree and the
**Vorbereitungsdienst**, and the Förster role then commonly comes with civil-servant status —
see [Beamte vs. Angestellte](/jobs-in-germany/reference/beamte-vs-angestellte/). This is the only
route in the repo that runs from a manual trade to Verbeamtung, and it runs through a
university, not through the trade.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

- **Holzernte** — felling, delimbing, cross-cutting, with chainsaw or by operating a
  harvester
- **Kulturbegründung** — planting, and increasingly **Waldumbau**: converting damaged
  monocultures to climate-resilient mixed stands
- **Jungbestandspflege** — thinning and tending young stands
- **Forstschutz** — bark beetle monitoring and sanitation felling
- Path and drainage maintenance, fencing, nature-conservation measures, occasional support
  for hunting operations

Mechanisation has changed the trade substantially: **harvester and forwarder operation** is
now a major part of it, and the operator roles are better paid and physically easier than
motor-manual work. Steep terrain still requires chainsaw felling.

## Safety is the defining condition {#safety-is-the-defining-condition}

Tree felling is among the most dangerous work done in Germany. Falling timber, spring-loaded
stems, chainsaw injuries and terrain accidents produce a serious injury and fatality rate
well above most occupations here.

Consequences that are not optional:
- **Motorsägenlehrgang** — documented chainsaw training to DGUV standards, in modules, with
  the higher modules required for storm-damaged and hung-up timber
- Full PPE: cut-protection trousers, helmet with visor and hearing protection, boots
- Working alone is restricted, and emergency communication is mandatory in many operations

**Foreign chainsaw certification does not transfer.** Expect to sit the German modules.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3 years dual**, forestry operation plus Berufsschule
- **Entry:** formally open; Hauptschul- or mittlerer Schulabschluss typical. Physical fitness
  is a real requirement.
- **Paid throughout** under the applicable public-sector training agreement where the
  employer is a state or municipal forest operation
- **Ladder:** **Forstwirtschaftsmeister**, **Forsttechniker**, harvester operator
  specialisation, or the degree route to Förster

## Pay {#pay}

Unusually for a manual trade, **much of this sector is public-sector and therefore
tariff-verified.** Which agreement applies depends on the owner:


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Employer | Agreement |
|---|---|
| Federal forests | TVöD Bund, TV-Wald-Bund |
| **State forests (Landesforsten)** | **TV-L / TV-L-Forst** (TV-H in Hessen) |
| Municipal forests | **TVöD VKA** |
| Private forests and contractors | No binding agreement — market rates, generally lower |

</div>


Figures below are **TVöD VKA**, valid **01.05.2026 – 31.03.2027**, verified 2026-09.
Typical grading is Forstwirt **EG 5–7**, Forstwirtschaftsmeister **EG 7–9**, Förster
**EG 9b–10**; grading varies by employer and duties.


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Role | Grade | Gross/month |
|---|---|---|
| Forstwirt/in, entry | EG 5 | €3,124 – €3,318 |
| Forstwirt/in, experienced | EG 6–7 | €3,681 – €4,045 |
| Forstwirtschaftsmeister/in | EG 7–9a | €3,295 – €4,980 |
| Förster/in (employed, Bachelor) | EG 9b–10 | €3,780 – €5,753 |
| Förster/in (verbeamtet) | gehobener Forstdienst, A 9–A 13 | €3,438 – €6,381 *(NRW)* |

</div>


The Beamten row is NRW Besoldung, valid 01.04.2026 – 28.02.2027; **Besoldung is state law**
and does not transfer between Bundesländer. Note that a verbeamteter Förster also nets
considerably more than the gross comparison suggests — see
[Beamte vs. Angestellte](/jobs-in-germany/reference/beamte-vs-angestellte/).

**Private forestry contractors (Forstunternehmen) pay below all of this**, and are where the
sector's precarious employment sits.

## Demand {#demand}

Strong, and driven by damage. The 2018–2020 droughts and the bark-beetle outbreak that
followed killed spruce stands across Germany on a scale that created a multi-decade
programme of clearance, replanting and **Waldumbau** toward climate-resilient mixed forest.
That work is funded, politically supported and long-running.

At the same time the workforce has shrunk for decades as state forest services cut manual
crews and outsourced to contractors. The result is genuine scarcity of qualified Forstwirte,
with the secure jobs concentrated in public employment and the precarious ones with
contractors.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work:** no recognition legally required — the title is not protected.
2. **For the visa:** equivalence assessment through the **Landwirtschaftskammer** or the
   state body, *not* IHK FOSA or the Handwerkskammer. Identify which applies in your target
   Bundesland first.
3. **Chainsaw certification must be German.** Budget for the modules.
4. **Pflanzenschutz-Sachkunde** is needed if your work involves applying pesticides.
5. **Language: B1–B2.** Safety briefings, felling coordination and emergency communication
   are in German, and this is not a trade where a misunderstanding is harmless.
6. **Visa:** skilled-worker route with recognition, or **§16a** to train here.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Public vs. contractor employment** is the largest single difference in pay, security and
  conditions. Establish which you are being offered.
- **Forstwirt is not Förster**, and no amount of field experience converts one into the
  other.
- **Physical wear and accident risk** are the profession's defining long-term problem. Plan a
  route toward machine operation, Meister or technician work.
- **Seasonality and weather** shape the year; storm damage means sudden, dangerous, urgent
  work.
