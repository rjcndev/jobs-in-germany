## One employer, not sixteen {#one-employer-not-sixteen}

[Police](/jobs-in-germany/public-service/polizist-in/), [fire services](/jobs-in-germany/public-service/feuerwehrmann-frau/), [teachers](/jobs-in-germany/education/lehrer-in/) and [Erzieher](/jobs-in-germany/education/erzieher-in/) are all fragmented across sixteen Länder with sixteen laws, sixteen recruitments and sixteen pay scales. **The Zoll is federal.** One employer, one application, one **Bundesbesoldung**, and deployment anywhere in Germany.

That is a real advantage — nothing to research state by state — and a real constraint: **you go where you are posted**, and a first posting is frequently not where you want to live.

## What the Zoll actually does {#what-the-zoll-actually-does}

Much more than airports. The service has several quite different arms, and which one you land in shapes the career:

| Area | Work |
|---|---|
| **Zollabfertigung** | Import and export clearance, tariffs, EU customs code, origin and valuation |
| **Verbrauchsteuern** | Excise duties — energy, tobacco, alcohol, coffee. A major revenue function |
| **Kfz-Steuer** | Vehicle tax, transferred to the Zoll in 2014 |
| **Kontrolleinheiten** | Mobile controls, goods and passenger checks, postal and freight |
| **FKS — Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit** | **Undeclared work enforcement** — see below |
| **Zollfahndung (ZKA)** | Criminal investigation: smuggling, money laundering, sanctions, organised crime |
| Artenschutz, Produktsicherheit | CITES, counterfeit goods, product safety at the border |

### The FKS is why this file matters to the rest of the repo {#the-fks-is-why-this-file-matters-to-the-rest-of-the-repo}

**Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit is the body that enforces the labour rules described throughout these files.** Minimum wage compliance, undeclared employment, **Scheinselbständigkeit** — the risk named in the [software](/jobs-in-germany/it/softwareentwickler-in/) and [hairdressing](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/friseur-in/) files — the **Werkvertrag ban in the meat industry** from the [butchery file](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/fleischer-in/), posted-worker rules in construction, and seasonal labour in [agriculture](/jobs-in-germany/green/).

If you have read this repo wondering who actually checks any of it: this is who. The FKS audits building sites, restaurants, meat plants, care services and harvests, and it has grown substantially since the statutory minimum wage arrived.

## Routes in {#routes-in}

Both are **paid from day one** as a Beamter auf Widerruf.

**Mittlerer Dienst (A 6 – A 9)**
- Entry: mittlerer Schulabschluss, or Hauptschulabschluss plus a completed Ausbildung
- **Two-year Vorbereitungsdienst**, theory at a Zoll training centre plus practical placements
- Graduate as **Zollsekretär/in**

**Gehobener Dienst (A 9 – A 13)**
- Entry: Abitur or Fachhochschulreife
- **Three-year dual Bachelor** (Diplom-Finanzwirt / LL.B.) at the **Hochschule des Bundes für öffentliche Verwaltung**, Fachbereich Finanzen
- Graduate as **Zollinspektor/in**

**Höherer Dienst** requires a law degree and a Referendariat.

Some roles — Kontrolleinheiten and Zollfahndung in particular — are **armed** and carry additional training and fitness requirements.

## Selection {#selection}

- Written aptitude test and interview or assessment centre
- **Sporttest** — required for the operational careers
- Medical examination, and a security/background check
- Age limits at appointment
- **Citizenship:** §7 BBG mirrors the Beamten rule — German, EU, EEA or Swiss nationality. Some sovereign-power and investigative roles are more tightly restricted; check for the specific career path.

## Pay {#pay}

**Bundesbesoldung — federal, so it is the same across Germany.** Figures below are the **last enacted table, valid 01.04.2025 – 30.04.2026**, verified 2026-09.

**An increase retroactive to 01.05.2026 is expected but was not yet enacted at the time of writing, so these figures are a floor rather than current.** This repo does not quote forecast tables — check the current Bundesbesoldung before relying on them.

| Stage | Grade | Gross/month |
|---|---|---|
| Zollsekretär/in (mittlerer Dienst) | A 6 – A 7 | €2,918 – €3,795 |
| Experienced, mittlerer Dienst | A 8 – A 9 | €3,217 – €4,412 |
| Zollinspektor/in (gehobener Dienst) | A 9 – A 10 | €3,455 – €4,918 |
| Zolloberinspektor/in, Zollamtsrat/-rätin | A 11 – A 12 | €4,179 – €5,989 |
| Senior posts | A 13 | €5,198 – €6,621 |

Anwärterbezüge apply during the Vorbereitungsdienst and are well below these figures. Supplements apply for shift, night and weekend duty in operational roles.

Beamte gross is **not comparable** to an employee's — see [Beamte vs. Angestellte](/jobs-in-germany/reference/beamte-vs-angestellte/).

## Demand {#demand}

The Zoll recruits in the thousands each year across both careers, and the FKS in particular has expanded with minimum-wage enforcement. This is one of the largest and most consistent public-sector recruiters in Germany.

## For non-German candidates {#for-non-german-candidates}

1. **Citizenship:** EU/EEA/Swiss nationals are eligible under §7 BBG for most paths — a meaningfully lower bar than [policing](/jobs-in-germany/public-service/polizist-in/), where several forces require German citizenship. Check the specific career.
2. **No recognition procedure.** A foreign customs qualification is not assessed; you enter the German Vorbereitungsdienst from the start.
3. **Language: C1.** The training is a law-heavy degree, decisions are legally binding acts, and documentation is evidential.
4. **Foreign-language skill is genuinely valued** operationally — but it is not a route in.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **You are posted where you are needed.** Nationwide deployment is the trade-off for nationwide uniformity.
- **The gehobener Dienst is a law degree.** People underestimate how academic the training is; it is not a practical customs course.
- **Which arm you land in matters enormously** — desk clearance work and mobile enforcement are very different jobs under one title.
- **The pay figures above are an expired table.** Check the enacted current one.
