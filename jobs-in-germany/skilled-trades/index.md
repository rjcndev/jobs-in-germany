Trades trained and regulated under the **Handwerksordnung (HwO)**, through the
**Handwerkskammer**. The industrial counterparts — often the same work, better paid, without
business rights — are in [industrial](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/).

## The rule that defines this sector {#the-rule-that-defines-this-sector}

**Employment is free. Self-employment is not.**

Trades in **Anlage A** of the HwO are considered hazardous or consumer-critical. To *work* in
one, a foreign-trained tradesperson needs no recognition at all. To **run a business** in
one, you must be entered in the **Handwerksrolle**, which normally requires the
**Meisterbrief** — or an equivalence decision, an **Ausnahmebewilligung (§8 HwO)**, or an
employed Betriebsleiter. EU/EEA nationals have an easier route under §9 HwO.

Trades in **Anlage B** carry no Meisterpflicht.

People are caught by this in both directions: waiting months for recognition they never
needed to be employed, or registering a Gewerbe and discovering they may not trade.

## Regulation moves in both directions {#regulation-moves-in-both-directions}

The 2004 reform deregulated dozens of trades. In **2020 the Meisterpflicht was restored for
twelve of them**, including Fliesenleger, Estrichleger, Parkettleger and Raumausstatter, with
existing businesses grandfathered — so one trade can contain owners under different rules
depending on when they started. Do not assume Anlage A membership is static.

## Pay, and the Handwerk discount {#pay-and-the-handwerk-discount}

Handwerk pay is **consistently below** the industrial equivalent — roughly €500/month at
entry across the electrical, metal and vehicle trades. See
[reference/pay.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/pay/). What the Handwerk route buys instead is the legal
path to **ownership**, which is where the real money in these trades is. An ageing generation
of owners with no successor makes **Betriebsübernahme** a realistic route for a Meister.

None of the Handwerk figures in this repo are tariff-verified; they are market estimates.

## Qualifications that gate the work {#qualifications-that-gate-the-work}

Several trades here are gated by something other than the job title — **Hochvolt** levels for
EV work, **DVGW** for gas, **DIN EN ISO 9606** welding certificates, **EN 1090** for
load-bearing structure. These rarely transfer from abroad and are often the real obstacle.

### Nahrungsmittelhandwerk {#nahrungsmittelhandwerk}

All three are Anlage A, all three need the §43 IfSG Infektionsschutz-Belehrung before first
handling food, and all three share the same sector shape: **collapsing business numbers and
an even faster collapse in trainee numbers**, so qualified staff are scarce inside a
shrinking trade. Counter sales is a separate occupation in each —
Fachverkäufer/in im Lebensmittelhandwerk.
