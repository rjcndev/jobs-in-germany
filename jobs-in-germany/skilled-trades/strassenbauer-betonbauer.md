> The two trades that build infrastructure rather than buildings. Written together because
> they share an Ausbildung family, a tariff world and — unusually for the Handwerk — an
> employer profile: **municipalities and large contractors, not small Betriebe.** Demand is
> driven by Germany's bridge, rail and grid programmes, which is a different economy from
> the housing market that governs the rest of the
> [Bauhauptgewerbe](/jobs-in-germany/reference/bauhauptgewerbe/).

## Two trades, one structure {#two-trades-one-structure}


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| | **Straßenbauer/in** | **Beton- und Stahlbetonbauer/in** |
|---|---|---|
| Builds | Roads, paths, squares, drainage, kerbs | Structures in in-situ and precast concrete |
| Core skills | Setting out and levels, base layers, **Asphalteinbau**, **Pflasterarbeiten**, edge and drainage detail | **Schalung** (formwork), **Bewehrung** (reinforcement), pouring, curing, finishing, precast erection |
| Typical projects | Municipal streets, motorway sections, cycle infrastructure, site access | Bridges, tunnels, retaining structures, industrial floors, wind-turbine foundations, multi-storey frames |
| Stage-2 qualification | **Tiefbaufacharbeiter/in** | **Hochbaufacharbeiter/in** |

</div>


Both run through the staged Bau Ausbildung described in [Maurer](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/maurer-in/): a common
first year, a recognised intermediate qualification after the second, the full trade after
the third, with much of the training funded through the **SOKA-BAU** levy.

**Pflasterarbeiten deserve a note of their own.** Laying setts and paving by hand is a
genuine craft, it is what most people picture when they think of this trade, and it is the
part most exposed to knee and back injury over a career.

## The employer difference {#the-employer-difference}

Most Handwerk trades in this repo mean a small firm with a Meister who owns it. These two
frequently do not:


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Employer | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Large contractors** (the national and international construction groups) | Tariff-bound, structured career paths, mobility across regions, long framework contracts |
| **Municipal Bauhof / Straßenbauamt** | **TVöD**, verifiable pay, regular hours, local work, a pension arrangement, and no winter uncertainty |
| **Autobahn GmbH and state road authorities** | Public employment on federal and state roads |
| Small specialist firms | Paving, drainage, private access works |

</div>


The municipal route is the underrated one. For anyone who wants construction work without
the away-site life, the working-time account and the winter Kurzarbeit, a **Bauhof** post is
tariff-paid, local, and on the verified TVöD scale — **EG 5–7, €3,124 – €4,045**
(valid 01.05.2026 – 31.03.2027) — with the security that goes with it.

## Hazards specific to these trades {#hazards-specific-to-these-trades}

- **Live traffic.** Working beside moving vehicles is the leading cause of death in road
  construction. Site protection under the German road-works rules is a trained,
  documented discipline, and the person setting out the signing carries responsibility for it
- **Hot asphalt** — burns, bitumen fume, and paving in summer heat
- **Teerhaltiger Straßenaufbruch** — old road layers containing coal tar are **carcinogenic**,
  and removing them is a notified procedure with mandatory protective measures. Never treat
  planings from an old road as inert
- **Quarzfeinstaub** from cutting and breaking concrete, the cause of silicosis, with
  dust-suppression obligations that are inspected
- **Hand-arm vibration** from breakers and compactors, and the noise that comes with them
- **Falls into excavations and trench collapse** in the drainage work

Most of these trades' people also hold [short
tickets](/jobs-in-germany/reference/occupational-certificates/) — earthmoving plant, forklift, traffic
management — which are quick, employer-funded, and the usual route to a pay step.

## Pay {#pay}

**Market estimate**, except the municipal row. The AEntG **Bau-Mindestlohn** is the floor and
is not quoted here.


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Tiefbau-/Hochbaufacharbeiter (after 2 years) | €2,800 – €3,300 |
| Qualified, entry | €3,100 – €3,800 |
| Experienced, with plant or formwork specialism | €3,700 – €4,500 |
| **Polier** | €4,200 – €5,300 |
| **Meister**, employed | €4,400 – €5,600 |
| **Municipal Bauhof (TVöD EG 5–7)** | **€3,124 – €4,045** |

</div>


Contractor work adds **Auslösung** and travel allowances — large projects mean long-distance
working and weeks away — plus the 13th month and SOKA-BAU's holiday and pension. Municipal
work trades those away for predictability.

## Demand and outlook {#demand-and-outlook}

**The strongest and most reliably funded demand in the whole construction section, and it is
not housing.**

- **Bridges.** Germany has a large, documented backlog of motorway and rail bridges requiring
  replacement or strengthening, with a multi-year programme and money behind it. This is the
  single biggest driver for Beton- und Stahlbetonbauer.
- **Rail.** The corridor renewal programme takes whole lines out of service and rebuilds
  them, which is concrete, track bed and road access all at once.
- **Grid and energy.** Transmission lines, substations, wind-turbine foundations.
- **Water and sewer renewal**, which is municipal, unglamorous and endless.

None of this depends on interest rates or housing demand the way the rest of the
Bauhauptgewerbe does — which makes these two trades the **counter-cyclical** choice inside a
sector that is otherwise having a difficult decade. The constraint is people: the shortage of
qualified Tiefbau workers is severe and is itself a reported reason for projects running late.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work: no recognition required.**
2. **For the visa: yes** — equivalence through the **Handwerkskammer**, or IHK FOSA where
   your qualification is an industrial one. Check which applies before filing.
3. **To run a business: the Meisterbrief**, or the alternatives in
   [the Meister file](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/).
4. **Language: B1–B2.** Setting out, levels, traffic-management briefings, and — for the
   municipal employers — dealing with residents.
5. **Visa:** §18a, **§16a**, or **§26(2) BeschV**.
6. **Plant and traffic-management certificates are German** and will be re-sat.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Contractor life means away-site life.** Ask how many weeks a year, and what the Auslösung
  actually is, before accepting.
- **The municipal route is easy to overlook** and is frequently the better job.
- **Old road surfaces can be carcinogenic.** If nobody has tested the layers before you break
  them out, that is a serious failure and not your risk to absorb.
- **Knees and back.** Paving and reinforcement fixing are the two hardest things on the body
  in this repo. Plan toward plant operation, Polier or Vermessung.
- **Winter differs by employer.** Municipal work continues — often as winter road service —
  while contractor work goes to Saison-Kurzarbeitergeld.
