> Fabricates and installs metal structures — gates, railings, staircases, balconies, facades, vehicle bodies. The Handwerk counterpart to [Konstruktionsmechaniker](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/konstruktionsmechaniker-in/), with lower pay and one thing the industrial trade cannot offer: a business of your own.

| **Historical term** | **Schlosser** — still in daily use, and in the word *Schlosserei* for the workshop |
| **Protected title** | Employment: no. Self-employment: yes — Handwerksordnung Anlage A |

## The pairing {#the-pairing}

| | **Metallbauer/in** | [**Konstruktionsmechaniker/in**](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/konstruktionsmechaniker-in/) |
|---|---|---|
| System | Handwerk, **HWK** | Industrie, **IHK** |
| Anlage A | **Yes — Meisterpflicht** | — |
| Self-employment | **Meisterbrief opens a business** | No business rights |
| Employer | Schlossereien, small metal shops | Stahlbau firms, shipyards, plant builders |
| Entry pay | €2,700 – €3,200 | €3,200 – €3,800 |
| Work | Building metalwork, customer-facing, varied | Structural steel, series and large fabrication |

Roughly €500/month apart at entry for related work — the same gap as [Elektroniker EGT vs Betriebstechnik](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/elektroniker-in-betriebstechnik/), and the same trade-off. The Handwerk route buys the legal path to ownership; the industrial route buys money now.

Which suits you depends on one question: **do you intend to run your own shop?** If yes, the Handwerk route is the direct one. If no, the industrial side pays better for comparable skill.

## Fachrichtungen {#fachrichtungen}

| Fachrichtung | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Konstruktionstechnik** | By far the most common: steel structures, staircases, railings, balconies, gates, metal doors and facades |
| **Metallgestaltung** | Artistic and architectural smithing, restoration of historic ironwork — the Kunstschmied tradition. A small, distinctive niche with correspondingly thin pay |
| **Nutzfahrzeugbau** | Commercial vehicle bodies, trailers, tipper and box bodies |

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Measuring on site, working from drawings, cutting and forming steel, stainless and aluminium, welding, assembling — and then **installing**, usually at the customer's building. Unlike the industrial trades, much of the day involves being in someone's house, shop or building site, so customer contact is constant and the work is highly varied: a staircase one week, a fire door the next.

**EN 1090 applies here too.** A Handwerk firm producing load-bearing structure — balconies, steel staircases, canopies — needs the same certified welding coordination and factory production control as an industrial fabricator. Small shops sometimes learn this uncomfortably late; if you plan to own one, it is a real cost of entry.

Welding qualifications to DIN EN ISO 9606 matter for the same reasons as in [Konstruktionsmechanik](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/konstruktionsmechaniker-in/), if usually across a narrower range of processes.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3.5 years dual**, Betrieb plus Berufsschule, **Handwerkskammer** exam
- **Entry:** formally open; Hauptschul- or mittlerer Schulabschluss typical
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €900–1,250/month gross, with wide regional variation
- **Ends with** the **Gesellenprüfung**
- **Then:** the **Metallbauermeister**, required to run a business, funded by **Aufstiegs-BAföG** with a state **Meisterprämie** in several Bundesländer

## Pay {#pay}

Handwerk levels — **market estimates**, not tariff-verified:

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Geselle, newly qualified | €2,700 – €3,200 |
| Geselle, experienced | €3,200 – €3,900 |
| With sought-after welding certificates | €3,500 – €4,200 |
| Metallbauermeister (employed) | €4,000 – €5,000 |
| Meister with own Schlosserei | Highly variable; a well-run shop outearns the employed ceiling |

As across the Handwerk, **ownership is the real earnings lever**, not seniority. Small metalworking shops with an order book and no successor are common, which makes **Betriebsübernahme** a realistic route for an ambitious Meister — the same dynamic as [SHK](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/anlagenmechaniker-in-shk/).

## Demand {#demand}

Solid and construction-linked: renovation, balcony and facade work, safety and fire-door installation, and metal substructures for solar and building-services retrofits. The workforce is ageing and small shops struggle to recruit, so bargaining power for a qualified Geselle is better than the pay levels suggest.

Metallgestaltung is the exception — genuinely niche, and best entered with eyes open.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To be employed:** no recognition legally required.
2. **To be self-employed:** Handwerksrolle entry, normally via the **Meisterbrief**, or an equivalence decision, an **Ausnahmebewilligung (§8 HwO)**, or an employed Betriebsleiter. EU/EEA nationals use the easier §9 HwO route.
3. **Equivalence** sits with the **Handwerkskammer** under the BQFG — not IHK FOSA.
4. **Welding certificates do not transfer**; expect to re-sit to EN standards.
5. **Language: B1–B2**, and realistically toward B2. This trade is in customers' buildings daily — measuring, explaining, agreeing changes — so it is more language-dependent than the industrial metal trades.
6. **Visa:** skilled-worker route with recognition, or **§16a** to train here.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Employment needs no recognition; self-employment does.** The standard Anlage A split.
- **EN 1090 certification** is a genuine cost for a small shop making load-bearing work.
- **The industrial side pays more** for comparable skill — know what you are trading away.
- **Fachrichtung shapes the career**: Metallgestaltung is artistically satisfying and economically thin.
- **"Schlosser" in an advert** is this trade, or sometimes industrial maintenance. Check.
