## Night work is the profession {#night-work-is-the-profession}

Bread has to be ready when the shop opens. Production therefore starts in the small hours —
commonly **02:00 to 04:00**, sometimes earlier — and the working day ends early afternoon.

This is not a detail to be negotiated away or grown out of. It shapes sleep, family life and
social contact permanently, and it is the single most common reason people leave the trade.
Anyone considering it should weigh that before the pay.

Night and Sunday work do attract supplements, which is why take-home exceeds the base
figures below.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Dough production and fermentation management, shaping, proving, baking, and **Feingebäck** —
pastries, Plundergebäck, seasonal work like Stollen and Christmas baking. Sourdough
management in quality bakeries is a genuine craft skill.

The honest caveat: in many chain bakeries a growing share of the work is **finishing
pre-made frozen product** rather than producing from raw ingredients. The distance between a
craft Bäckerei and a bake-off operation is large, and both advertise for Bäcker.

**§43 IfSG Infektionsschutz-Belehrung**, HACCP and allergen labelling apply throughout.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3 years dual**, Betrieb plus Berufsschule, **Handwerkskammer** exam
- **Entry:** formally open
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €900–1,200/month gross
- **Ends with** the **Gesellenprüfung**
- **Then:** the **Bäckermeister** for ownership, funded by
  [Aufstiegs-BAföG](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/); also **Brotsommelier**, a genuine if niche
  further qualification that has helped quality bakeries differentiate

Counter sales is a separate occupation: **Fachverkäufer/in im Lebensmittelhandwerk**,
Fachrichtung Bäckerei.

## Demand — severe shortage, shrinking sector {#demand--severe-shortage-shrinking-sector}

The number of Handwerk bakeries has fallen for decades, from tens of thousands to a small
fraction of that. Industrial bread and supermarket bake-off took the volume; **energy costs
in 2022–23** pushed many marginal businesses over; and succession failure closed more.

And yet **training numbers have collapsed faster than the sector has**, so qualified bakers
are genuinely scarce. Employers compete for Gesellen and cannot find Meister to take over
businesses.

Same reading as [butchery](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/fleischer-in/): employment is easy to find, the sector is not
growing, and ownership opportunities are going unfilled for want of anyone qualified to take
them.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To be employed:** no recognition legally required.
2. **To be self-employed:** Handwerksrolle entry — Meisterbrief, equivalence decision,
   **§7b Altgesellenregelung**, **§8 Ausnahmebewilligung**, or an employed Betriebsleiter.
   See [meister.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/).
3. **Equivalence** sits with the **Handwerkskammer**.
4. **Language: B1.** Production work is less language-dependent than customer-facing trades,
   which makes this a realistic entry occupation — but B2 is needed for the Meister route.
5. **Visa:** skilled-worker route with recognition, or **§16a** to train here.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **The hours.** Stated plainly above, and the main cause of attrition.
- **Craft bakery vs bake-off chain** — very different work, same job title in adverts. Ask
  what proportion is produced from scratch.
- **Physical**: heat, heavy dough handling, flour dust. **Bäckerasthma** (flour-dust
  sensitisation) is a recognised occupational disease and a real career-ending risk;
  extraction and handling practice matter.
- **Employment needs no recognition; self-employment does.**
