## Not quite Bauhauptgewerbe, not quite Ausbau {#not-quite-bauhauptgewerbe-not-quite-ausbau}

Roofing sits structurally inside the construction world described in
[The Bauhauptgewerbe](/jobs-in-germany/reference/bauhauptgewerbe/) — a sector holiday fund, an AEntG
minimum wage above the statutory one, a funded winter — but **under its own agreements**, not
the BRTV-Bau.


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| | Roofing |
|---|---|
| Framework agreement | The **Dachdeckerhandwerk** agreements, not BRTV-Bau |
| Social fund | **SOKA-DACH** — its own Urlaubskasse and supplementary pension, same principle as SOKA-BAU |
| Minimum wage | Its **own AEntG-declared** rate, applying nationwide |
| Winter | **Saison-Kurzarbeitergeld** applies, as in construction |

</div>


**So check which agreement a contract names.** "Construction" is not one tariff world, and a
Dachdecker on a Bau contract or vice versa is in the wrong scheme.

## Specialisations {#specialisations}

Certified **on the qualification** — you choose a Fachrichtung during the Ausbildung:


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Fachrichtung | Work |
|---|---|
| **Dach-, Wand- und Abdichtungstechnik** | The main one: pitched and flat roofs, waterproofing |
| **Energietechnik an Dach und Wand** | **Solar thermal and photovoltaic mounting**, roof-integrated systems, insulation |
| **Außenwandbekleidungstechnik** | Facade cladding |
| **Reetdachtechnik** | Thatch — a tiny, regionally concentrated specialism on the North Sea and Baltic coasts |

</div>


The Energietechnik Fachrichtung is the reason this trade has a growth story rather than a
steady one.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Pitched roofs in tile, slate and metal; flat roofs in bitumen and single-ply membrane;
**Dachabdichtung** and detailing at every penetration; **Dämmung** to GEG standards;
guttering and rainwater; facade cladding; **green roofs**; and increasingly **PV mounting
systems** and the roof build-up that has to stay watertight around them.

Renovation dominates new-build in this trade, and storm damage produces sudden urgent work.

**The PV boundary is worth understanding.** Mounting the array, penetrating and sealing the
roof and carrying the structural load is Dachdecker work; the **electrical connection** is
[electrical](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/elektroniker-in-energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/) work and is gated by
[DGUV V3 and the EFK rules](/jobs-in-germany/reference/occupational-certificates/). Firms increasingly
cover both by employing both trades, and the division of responsibility on a job is a
frequent source of dispute. Know where your own competence — and your insurance — stops.

## Safety is the trade {#safety-is-the-trade}

Roofing has one of the worst fall-accident records in German working life, and
**Absturzsicherung** is not administrative overhead here; it is the daily structure of the
work.

- **Collective protection first** — scaffolding, edge protection, safety nets. Personal
  fall-arrest (**PSAgA**) is the fallback, not the default, and using it requires
  documented training and an anchor point that has been assessed
- **Rescue planning is mandatory** where PSAgA is used: a person hanging in a harness must be
  recovered within minutes
- **Weather stops work.** Wind, ice and wet surfaces are hard limits, not judgement calls
- **Long-term exposure** — UV, hot bitumen, mineral fibres, asbestos in older roofs, and the
  knee and shoulder wear of a career on pitched surfaces

**BG BAU** is the accident insurer, and in this trade its rules are enforced hard and
inspected.

## Pay {#pay}

**Market estimate**, positioned against the roofing tariff. The sector's own AEntG minimum is
the floor and is a perishable figure — check the current rate rather than trusting one
written down.


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Geselle, entry | €3,000 – €3,600 |
| Experienced | €3,500 – €4,200 |
| Vorarbeiter / Kolonnenführer | €4,000 – €4,800 |
| **Meister**, employed | €4,300 – €5,400 |
| Meister with own business | Not comparable |

</div>


Add the sector's holiday and supplementary-pension entitlements through **SOKA-DACH**, travel
and away-site allowances, and — in a trade with this much emergency work — overtime that is
genuinely available. Subtract the winter: **Saison-Kurzarbeitergeld** months are paid at a
replacement rate, not at full wage.

## Demand and outlook {#demand-and-outlook}

**Among the strongest in this repo, and for three independent reasons at once.**

1. **Energy retrofit.** Roof insulation is one of the highest-return measures under the GEG,
   and it is funded.
2. **Photovoltaics.** Germany's rooftop PV expansion runs through this trade, and the
   Fachrichtung exists precisely for it.
3. **Storm and hail damage**, increasing with the climate, produces repair work that cannot
   be deferred.

Against that: the residential new-build contraction affects roofing as it affects everything
else, and roofing firms are small, so an individual employer's order book matters more than
the national picture. The **shortage of qualified roofers is severe** — entrant numbers are
low relative to demand, which is why training pay and Geselle rates have risen faster here
than in most trades.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work: no recognition required.** Anlage A binds only self-employment.
2. **For the visa: yes** — **Handwerkskammer** equivalence for §18a.
3. **To run a business: the Meisterbrief**, or the routes in
   [the Meister file](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/), including **§7b**.
4. **Language: B1–B2.** Safety coordination at height is not a place for ambiguity, and
   customer contact on domestic roofs is constant.
5. **Visa:** §18a, **§16a** to train here, or **§26(2) BeschV**.
6. **Fall-protection and PSAgA training is German and does not transfer.** Expect to sit it.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Check the agreement and the social fund.** Roofing is SOKA-DACH, not SOKA-BAU, and
  ending up in neither is a real risk with a small employer.
- **The PV boundary cuts both ways** — doing electrical work you are not qualified for is
  both illegal and uninsured.
- **Winter is part of the deal.** Ask how the employer handles the Schlechtwetterzeit before
  accepting: working-time account, Saison-Kug, or — the wrong answer — a termination.
- **The career is physically finite.** Meister, Kolonnenführer, surveying and estimating, or
  the PV side, are the routes that keep you in the trade past forty-five.
- **Asbestos in older roofs** requires a separate qualification and a notified procedure.
  Never take it on informally.
