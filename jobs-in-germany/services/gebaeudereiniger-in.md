> **The largest Handwerk trade in Germany by headcount**, and absent from this repo until now. It is **Anlage B1** — no Meisterpflicht — which makes it a useful contrast with the Anlage A trades that fill the [skilled-trades](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/) folder. It also has its own **AEntG-declared minimum wage** above the statutory one, and it is the sector where outsourcing, minimum-wage compliance and migrant labour intersect most visibly.

## Anlage B1 — what it means to be a trade without a Meisterpflicht {#anlage-b1--what-it-means-to-be-a-trade-without-a-meisterpflicht}

The [skilled trades](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/) in this repo are **Anlage A**: employment free, self-employment requiring the Meisterbrief. Gebäudereiniger is **Anlage B1** — *zulassungsfrei*. Anyone may register the trade and open a cleaning business.

| | **Anlage A** (e.g. [Elektroniker EGT](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/elektroniker-in-energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/)) | **Anlage B1** (Gebäudereiniger) |
|---|---|---|
| Employment | Free | Free |
| **Self-employment** | **Meisterbrief required** | **Free** |
| Ausbildung | Yes, 3–3.5 years | Yes, 3 years |
| Meister exists? | Yes, and it is the gate | **Yes, and it is voluntary** |
| Who may train apprentices | A Meister | A Meister or otherwise qualified trainer |

**The consequence is the sector you see.** Barrier-free entry to self-employment produces very large numbers of very small firms, intense price competition on contracts, and — at the bottom of the market — the compliance problems this file and [Reinigungskraft](/jobs-in-germany/services/reinigungskraft/) describe.

The Meisterbrief is still worth having. It is what separates an Objektleiter from a business owner who can bid for hospital, cleanroom and facade contracts, and it carries the training right.

## The trade is not "cleaning" {#the-trade-is-not-cleaning}

The three-year Ausbildung exists because a large part of this work is technical, hazardous or both:

- **Unterhaltsreinigung** — maintenance cleaning of offices, schools and public buildings. The volume end
- **Glas- und Fassadenreinigung** — including **work at height**: rope access, cradles, facade access systems. A separate and better-paid Lohngruppe, with the [fall-protection requirements](/jobs-in-germany/reference/occupational-certificates/) that implies
- **Sonderreinigung** — post-construction, fire and water damage, graffiti removal, industrial plant
- **Hygienereinigung** in hospitals and food production — disinfection procedure, surface classification, infection control. This is where errors have consequences
- **Reinraumreinigung** — semiconductor and pharmaceutical cleanrooms, with protocol and documentation
- **PV-Anlagen, Verkehrsmittel, Denkmalpflege** — increasingly specialised niches

Chemistry, surfaces, machines, water treatment, hazard assessment and documentation are the substance of the qualification. A trained Gebäudereiniger and an untrained Reinigungskraft are not doing the same job, even where the job title on the rota is the same.

## Pay {#pay}

The sector's **AEntG-declared minimum wage** binds every employer, including foreign firms operating here, and sits **above** the statutory Mindestlohn. It has **Lohngruppen** — the interior-cleaning group and a higher one for **glass and facade** work.

**Both are perishable figures and are not quoted here.** Check the current rates with the Bundesinnungsverband or the Zoll's FKS pages.

| Stage | Gross/month — **market estimate** |
|---|---|
| Qualified Gebäudereiniger/in, maintenance cleaning | €2,400 – €2,900 |
| **Glas- und Fassadenreinigung** | €2,800 – €3,400 |
| Specialist — cleanroom, hygiene, Sonderreinigung | €3,000 – €3,600 |
| **Objektleiter/in** | €3,200 – €4,200 |
| **Meister/in**, employed | €3,800 – €4,800 |
| Fachwirt / Hygienemanagement, larger contractor | €4,200 – €5,500 |

**Full-time is the exception in this sector**, which is the single most important thing the monthly figures hide. A large share of the workforce is part-time or on a [Minijob](/jobs-in-germany/reference/minijob-und-geringfuegige-beschaeftigung/), and a monthly figure assumes hours that many contracts do not provide.

## Demand and outlook {#demand-and-outlook}

**Permanent, universal and recession-resistant.** Every building is cleaned; the work cannot be offshored and is only marginally automatable — cleaning robots handle open floor area in large buildings and do not touch the rest.

Where the growth is: **hygiene and infection control**, which gained permanent attention after 2020; **cleanroom** work, as semiconductor and pharmaceutical capacity is built in Germany; and **facade and PV cleaning**.

The honest picture of the sector: it runs on **outsourcing**. Hospitals, hotels — see [Hotelfachmann/-frau](/jobs-in-germany/hospitality/hotelfachmann-frau/), which already notes that housekeeping is usually contracted out — offices and schools buy cleaning as a service, and contracts are won on price. That pressure lands on hours, staffing levels and compliance, and it is why the **Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit** treats this sector as a priority.

Being *qualified* is the way out of the bottom of it. The trade's own problem is that very few of the people working in cleaning hold the qualification.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work: no recognition required**, and no qualification required at all for the unqualified tier — see [Reinigungskraft](/jobs-in-germany/services/reinigungskraft/).
2. **For the visa: the qualification matters.** §18a needs a recognised equivalent, assessed by the **Handwerkskammer**. Unqualified cleaning work supports **no skilled-worker permit**.
3. **To open a business: nothing is required** beyond the ordinary trade registration — Anlage B1. This is one of the most accessible routes to self-employment in this repo, and it is used as such.
4. **Language: A2–B1 for the work**, B2 to supervise. Hazard communication, chemical labelling and hygiene protocols are the parts where it matters.
5. **Visa:** §18a with recognition, **§16a** to train here, or **§26(2) BeschV**.
6. **The AEntG minimum applies to you regardless of nationality, contract law or posting.** If you are being paid less, that is enforceable.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Check which Lohngruppe you are in.** Facade and glass work is a higher rate, and people are routinely paid the interior rate for it.
- **Hours are the real variable**, not the hourly rate. Ask what is contractually guaranteed.
- **Travel between sites** is frequently unpaid in practice and frequently should not be. Working time includes what the law says it includes, not what the rota says.
- **Being registered as self-employed by a contractor** is the pattern to refuse — see [Fliesenleger](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/fliesenleger-in/) for the same mechanism in another trade.
- **The qualification is genuinely worth money here**, more than in most trades, because so few people in the sector hold it.
