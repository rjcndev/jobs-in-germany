The occupations here belong to none of the chamber-defined sectors that organise the rest of
this repo. Some are Handwerk, some IHK, some have **no qualification at all** — and that
last group is the reason this folder exists.

## Files with no qualification to write about {#files-with-no-qualification-to-write-about}

Most files in this repo are built around a qualification: what it is, how long it takes, who
examines it, and how a foreign equivalent is assessed. Two of the files here —
[Reinigungskraft](/jobs-in-germany/services/reinigungskraft/) and [Haushaltshilfe](/jobs-in-germany/services/haushaltshilfe/) — have none of
that, because **the work requires no qualification and therefore has no recognition and no
equivalence step.**

Leaving them out would have been the easy choice, and the wrong one: between them they are
one of the most common first jobs for people arriving in Germany. So the frame changes.
Instead of qualification and recognition, those files are about **employment status, minimum
wage and enforcement** — which is what actually determines whether the work is decent or not.

## Cleaning is three situations, not one {#cleaning-is-three-situations-not-one}

| Tier | What it is |
|---|---|
| **[Gebäudereiniger/in](/jobs-in-germany/services/gebaeudereiniger-in/)** | The skilled trade. Three-year Ausbildung, HWK examination, **Anlage B1** — so no Meisterpflicht. A real profession with a ladder to Objektleitung and Meister |
| **[Reinigungskraft](/jobs-in-germany/services/reinigungskraft/)** | **No qualification.** Employed by cleaning contractors, paid the AEntG sector minimum, frequently part-time, Minijob or agency. Most of the sector by headcount |
| **[Haushaltshilfe](/jobs-in-germany/services/haushaltshilfe/)** | Private households. The narrowest case and the one with the real legal story: **the overwhelming majority of it is undeclared** |

Conflating them — as English usually does, with one word — hides the fact that they sit under
different law, different pay floors and different enforcement.

## What else is here {#what-else-is-here}

- **[Wissenschaftliche/r Mitarbeiter/in](/jobs-in-germany/services/wissenschaftliche-r-mitarbeiter-in/)** — German
  academia, and the **WissZeitVG**, which exists to permit serial fixed-term contracts for
  years where ordinary employment law would not.
- **[Fachkraft für Veranstaltungstechnik](/jobs-in-germany/services/fachkraft-fuer-veranstaltungstechnik/)** — event
  and stage technology: IHK rather than Handwerk, with a **legally required competent person**
  in every larger venue.

## The way up {#the-way-up}

Both unqualified tiers have a route out, and it is worth stating where the files can be seen
together: **[Gebäudereiniger/in](/jobs-in-germany/services/gebaeudereiniger-in/)** for the cleaning side, and
**[Hauswirtschafter/in](/jobs-in-germany/green/hauswirtschafter-in/)** — classified as a green profession
but employed in care homes, schools and institutions — for anyone already doing catering,
laundry and household work in an institution. Both are reachable by funded **Umschulung** or,
with enough documented experience, by **Externenprüfung**.

## Common threads {#common-threads}

- **[Minijob and geringfügige Beschäftigung](/jobs-in-germany/reference/minijob-und-geringfuegige-beschaeftigung/)**
  is the background law for most of this folder, and it is where the pension opt-out trap
  and the Haushaltsscheck procedure are explained.
- **[Employment basics](/jobs-in-germany/reference/employment-basics/)** matters more here than
  anywhere else in the repo, because these are the jobs where statutory rights are most often
  simply not given.
- **Enforcement** in several of these sectors is by the **Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit**,
  the customs unit staffed by the [Zollbeamte](/jobs-in-germany/public-service/zollbeamte-r/) profiled
  elsewhere here.
