## Who employs you decides almost everything {#who-employs-you-decides-almost-everything}

| Employer | Pay floor | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **A cleaning contractor** (Gebäudereinigungsfirma) | The **AEntG Gebäudereiniger minimum**, above the statutory Mindestlohn | The normal case. The sector minimum binds the employer whatever the contract says |
| **The building owner directly** | Statutory **Mindestlohn** | Less common; sometimes better hours |
| **A public employer** — school, municipality, hospital | **TVöD**, lowest groups | Rare and good: tariff pay, 30 days' leave, a pension arrangement |
| **A temp agency** (Zeitarbeit) | Agency tariff, then **equal pay** after nine months | Check when equal pay starts |
| **"Self-employed", invoicing the contractor** | — | **Refuse this.** It is the Scheinselbstständigkeit pattern; you lose every right below |

**The first row is the one that matters most.** The cleaning sector's minimum wage is
declared generally binding under the **AEntG**, which means it applies to every employer
operating in Germany — including a foreign company posting workers here — and it is **above**
the general minimum wage. It has a higher group for **glass and facade** work.

It is a perishable figure, so it is not quoted here. Look it up before you accept a job:
the Zoll publishes the current rates, and the Zoll is also who enforces them.

## What you are owed, and what is routinely withheld {#what-you-are-owed-and-what-is-routinely-withheld}

All of these apply to unqualified cleaning work, including part-time and
[Minijob](/jobs-in-germany/reference/minijob-und-geringfuegige-beschaeftigung/) contracts:

| Entitlement | Frequently denied by |
|---|---|
| **The sector minimum wage, for every hour worked** | Under-recorded hours |
| **Paid holiday**, pro rata to days worked | Being told Minijobs have none — they do |
| **Continued pay when sick**, after four weeks' service | The same claim |
| **Pay for travel between sites during a shift** | Treating it as your own time — **it is working time** |
| **Written recording of all hours** | Simply not doing it |
| **A written contract with the essential terms** | Verbal arrangements |

> **Leistungsvorgaben are where this goes wrong in practice.** Contracts are won on price,
> so cleaners are given square-metre-per-hour targets that cannot be met in the hours paid.
> The hours then get worked and not recorded. That is not a grey area: it is unpaid work, and
> the hour records are the evidence. **Keep your own.**

Working-time records in cleaning are a legal obligation under the MiLoG and are the first
thing a **Finanzkontrolle Schwarzarbeit** inspection asks for. The sector is one of the FKS's
declared priorities — which is useful to know, because it means the enforcement machinery
exists and is used.

## The shape of the work {#the-shape-of-the-work}

Early mornings and evenings, around the occupants of the building; split shifts; multiple
sites in one day; part-time by default. Offices, schools, clinics, stations, factories,
stairwells.

Physically it is repetitive strain more than heavy lifting — shoulders, wrists, knees, back —
and the chemical exposure is real enough that hazard information and protective equipment are
employer obligations, not extras.

## Pay {#pay}

**Market estimate**, anchored to the sector minimum.

| | Gross/month, full-time equivalent |
|---|---|
| Interior cleaning, sector minimum | €2,100 – €2,400 |
| Glass and facade group | €2,500 – €3,000 |
| Experienced, with responsibility for a site | €2,600 – €3,100 |
| **Public employer (TVöD, lowest groups)** | **€2,700 – €3,100** |

**Almost nobody in this occupation works full time.** Part-time and Minijob contracts
dominate, so the realistic monthly figure is a fraction of the above. That is the single most
important number in this file, and it is why cleaning is so often a second job.

## The way out is the qualification {#the-way-out-is-the-qualification}

This is the most useful thing in this file. The unqualified tier has no ladder; the
qualified trade does.

- **[Gebäudereiniger/in](/jobs-in-germany/services/gebaeudereiniger-in/)** — the three-year Ausbildung, or an
  **Umschulung** for adults, typically compressed to two years
- **Teilqualifizierung (TQ)** — modular partial qualifications, each a few months, that
  stack toward the full qualification and can end in an **Externenprüfung** for people with
  enough documented work experience
- Both are commonly funded by a
  **[Bildungsgutschein](/jobs-in-germany/reference/weiterbildung-funding/)**, and cleaning is a sector
  where the Agentur funds this readily
- The qualification roughly doubles the ceiling and opens **Objektleitung** and, with the
  Meister, running a business — which in this **Anlage B1** trade requires no licence at all

## For people who have just arrived {#for-people-who-have-just-arrived}

1. **This work is legal, available everywhere, and it will not get you a visa.** Unqualified
   cleaning supports **no skilled-worker permit**. If immigration status is your concern,
   treat this as income while you qualify for something else, not as a route.
2. **Check what your residence permit allows** before taking a job or a second one. Many
   permits restrict or condition employment, and a Minijob alongside another job is not
   automatically permitted.
3. **Language: A2 is workable, B1 makes you employable on better sites.** Hazard labels,
   chemical instructions and hygiene protocols are German, and the step to supervision
   requires more.
4. **You are owed the sector minimum wage whatever your nationality, and whoever your
   employer is.** Posting from abroad does not lower it.
5. **Do not accept being registered as self-employed** to do cleaning work someone else
   directs. It removes the minimum wage, the holiday, the sick pay and the accident cover.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Unrecorded hours are the sector's defining problem.** Keep a private log with dates,
  sites and times. It is worth money later.
- **Ask which minimum applies** — the general one or the higher cleaning one. If the employer
  cannot say, that is informative.
- **Travel time between objects during a shift is working time.**
- **The Minijob pension opt-out** costs you more than it gives you; see
  [the Minijob reference](/jobs-in-germany/reference/minijob-und-geringfuegige-beschaeftigung/).
- **A public-sector cleaning job is a genuinely different job** at the same work. They are
  advertised, and they are worth applying for.
