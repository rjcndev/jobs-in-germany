One regulated sector, several job titles. Door supervision, site guarding, patrols, retail protection and event security are **roles inside it**, not separate occupations.

## §34a GewO — the gate {#34a-gewo--the-gate}

Private security is **activity-gated**, like [BaFin registration](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/bankkaufmann-frau/) for investment advice and the [driving licence](/jobs-in-germany/logistics/berufskraftfahrer-in/) for professional driving. The job title is unprotected; what you may *do* is not.

Three tiers, and which applies depends on the work:

| Tier | What it is | Required for |
|---|---|---|
| **Unterrichtung** | A **40-hour IHK instruction course**, attendance-based, no exam | Basic guarding: site protection, reception, most static roles |
| **Sachkundeprüfung §34a** | An **IHK examination** — written and oral, covering law, powers and conduct | **Door supervision at licensed venues**, patrols in public space, retail detectives, refugee accommodation, protected large events, and **anyone running a security business** |
| **Ausbildung** | 2-year Servicekraft or **3-year [Fachkraft für Schutz und Sicherheit](/jobs-in-germany/security/fachkraft-fuer-schutz-und-sicherheit/)** | Nothing extra legally — but it is the route to supervision and better pay |

On top of all three: a **Zuverlässigkeitsüberprüfung** (reliability check), and entry in the **Bewacherregister**, mandatory since 2019. Employers must verify registration before deploying you.

**The Sachkundeprüfung is a German-language legal exam** — BGB, StGB, StPO, GewO, data protection, use-of-force limits. It is the real barrier for foreign applicants, not the work.

## What the exam teaches, and why it matters {#what-the-exam-teaches-and-why-it-matters}

Security staff in Germany have **no special powers**. They act on the same rights as any citizen — Notwehr, Nothilfe, the citizen's arrest right under §127 StPO — plus whatever the property owner may delegate through Hausrecht. Exceeding that is an offence committed by you personally.

That is the substance of the Sachkundeprüfung and the reason it exists.

## Pay {#pay}

The sector has **regional collective agreements** (BDSW / ver.di) and an **AEntG-declared branch minimum wage above the statutory minimum**, historically differentiated by Bundesland and by activity, with aviation security highest. Rates change and have been converging — **check the current figures** rather than trusting any written here.

All pay figures in these files are **market estimates**.
