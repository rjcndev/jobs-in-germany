## The roles, and what each one needs {#the-roles-and-what-each-one-needs}

| Role | German | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Site / building guard, night guard | **Objektschutz**, Werkschutz, Pförtner | Unterrichtung |
| Mobile patrol | **Revierdienst**, Streifendienst | Unterrichtung |
| **Door supervisor / bouncer** | **Türsteher**, Einlasskontrolle | **Sachkundeprüfung** |
| Retail loss prevention | **Kaufhausdetektiv** | **Sachkundeprüfung** |
| Patrols in public space | Kontrollgänge im öffentlichen Verkehrsraum | **Sachkundeprüfung** |
| Refugee accommodation | Schutz von Gemeinschaftsunterkünften | **Sachkundeprüfung** |
| Large protected events | Großveranstaltungen mit Zugangsschutz | **Sachkundeprüfung** |
| Aviation security screening | **Luftsicherheitskontrollkraft**, §5 LuftSiG | Separate federal certification |

**Door work is not an entry-level job in Germany.** Working the door of a club or bar requires the **Sachkundeprüfung** — the IHK examination, not the 40-hour course. This surprises people with door experience from countries where the barrier is lower.

**"Nachtwächter" is a historical word.** The modern job is Objektschutz or Revierdienst; the old term now mostly denotes the costumed night-watchman tours in medieval town centres. Recorded here the way this repo records [Schlosser](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/metallbauer-in/) and [Lehrling](/jobs-in-germany/reference/ausbildung/).

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Access control and identity checks, patrol rounds and alarm response, CCTV monitoring, reception and key management, incident documentation, fire-safety and first-aid duties, and de-escalation. The written **Meldung** after an incident matters more than most newcomers expect — it is evidence.

**You have no special powers.** See the [sector README](/jobs-in-germany/security/): you act on ordinary citizen rights plus delegated Hausrecht, and exceeding them is your personal offence.

## Conditions, plainly {#conditions-plainly}

Nights, weekends and 12-hour shifts are standard. Much of it is **lone working**, often at remote sites. Door and public-facing roles carry a real **assault risk**, and assaults on security staff are common enough to be a sector-wide occupational issue. Turnover is very high.

Night-shift supplements apply under §6 ArbZG and are a meaningful part of take-home.

## Pay {#pay}

**Market estimates.** The AEntG branch minimum for the Sicherheitsgewerbe sits above the statutory minimum and varies by region and activity — see the [sector README](/jobs-in-germany/security/):

| Role | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Objektschutz, Unterrichtung only | €2,200 – €2,800 |
| With §34a Sachkunde | €2,400 – €3,100 |
| Türsteher (experienced, busy venue) | €2,600 – €3,400 |
| **Luftsicherheitskontrollkraft** | **€3,200 – €4,000** |
| Objektleitung / Einsatzleitung | €3,300 – €4,300 |

Aviation security is the best-paid end and has its own federal certification and background checks — worth targeting deliberately.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work:** no professional recognition needed, but **§34a compliance is mandatory** — Unterrichtung or Sachkundeprüfung, the reliability check, and Bewacherregister entry.
2. **The reliability check** requires a clean record; for non-Germans that means a certificate of good conduct from your home country as well.
3. **The Sachkundeprüfung is in German** and is a legal exam. This, not the physical work, is the barrier — and it is what separates €2,400 roles from €3,000 ones.
4. **For the skilled-worker visa:** as with [bartending](/jobs-in-germany/hospitality/barkeeper-in/), an Unterrichtung is not a qualification. §18a needs a recognised occupation — the [3-year Fachkraft](/jobs-in-germany/security/fachkraft-fuer-schutz-und-sicherheit/), or something else. **Westbalkanregelung (§26(2) BeschV)** is widely used in this sector.
5. **Language: B1–B2.** Incident reports, legal limits and de-escalation are all in German.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Assuming door work is entry-level.** It needs the Sachkundeprüfung.
- **Assuming you have powers you do not.** Overstepping is a personal criminal matter.
- **Unterrichtung is not a qualification** for visa purposes.
- **Check who pays for the Unterrichtung or Sachkunde** — reputable employers fund it; some deduct it, or bind you with a repayment clause.
- **Lone working at night** — confirm what emergency contact and backup actually exist.
