The single most repeated paragraph across the profession files, collected once. **There is no national recognition office in Germany.** Which body is competent depends on your profession, and for most of them also on your Bundesland.

**Start here:** [anerkennung-in-deutschland.de](https://www.anerkennung-in-deutschland.de) — the official portal, multilingual, and its finder names the competent body for your profession and target state. Everything below explains what it will tell you and why.

## Three questions decide everything {#three-questions-decide-everything}

**1. Is the profession regulated?**

If yes — the licensed healthcare professions, teaching, and the Kammer professions — **recognition is mandatory before you may work at all**. No licence, no job.

If no — most trades, IT, commercial, logistics, hospitality — **you may work immediately**. Recognition is then needed only for the **skilled-worker visa**, not for the work. That distinction is covered in [visa routes](/jobs-in-germany/reference/visa-routes/) and catches people constantly.

**2. Is it a Beamtenberuf?**

Then **no recognition procedure exists at all.** You do not bring a foreign police, customs or tax qualification to be assessed — you enter the German training from scratch, and citizenship is the gate. See [Beamte vs. Angestellte](/jobs-in-germany/reference/beamte-vs-angestellte/).

**3. Does the occupation exist in Germany?**

If not — [bartending](/jobs-in-germany/hospitality/barkeeper-in/) is the clear case — there is nothing to be assessed as equivalent *to*, and the skilled-worker route is simply unavailable.

## The four families of competent body {#the-four-families-of-competent-body}

### 1. The three chamber systems {#1-the-three-chamber-systems}

| Chamber | Covers | In this repo |
|---|---|---|
| **IHK FOSA** — on behalf of the Industrie- und Handelskammern | Commercial, industrial, IT, logistics, hospitality occupations | The [industrial trades](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/), [commercial](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/), [hospitality](/jobs-in-germany/hospitality/), [logistics](/jobs-in-germany/logistics/) |
| **Handwerkskammer (HWK)** | Handwerk trades under the HwO | [All skilled trades](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/) |
| **Landwirtschaftskammer** | Agriculture, forestry, horticulture — **and only in some Bundesländer**; elsewhere a state ministry | [Grüne Berufe](/jobs-in-germany/green/) |

Most people know the first two exist and are surprised by the third. Note also that the **same trade can sit in different systems**: a [Handwerk electrician](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/elektroniker-in-energie-und-gebaeudetechnik/) goes to the HWK, an [industrial one](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/elektroniker-in-betriebstechnik/) to IHK FOSA.

### 2. Kammern of the freie Berufe {#2-kammern-of-the-freie-berufe}

Self-governing professional bodies, organised **per Bundesland**:

| Kammer | Profession |
|---|---|
| **Landesärztekammer** | [Doctors](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/arzt-aerztin/) — the Fachsprachprüfung, and Facharzt recognition |
| **Apothekerkammer** | [Pharmacists](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/apotheker-in/) — Fachsprachprüfung |
| **Rechtsanwaltskammer** | [Legal secretaries](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/rechtsanwaltsfachangestellte-r/) — note: **not** IHK FOSA, because this is a freier-Beruf occupation |
| **Steuerberaterkammer** | [Tax clerks](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/steuerfachangestellte-r/) — likewise |
| **Architektenkammer** | Architects — entry in the Architektenliste |
| **Ingenieurkammer** | [Engineers](/jobs-in-germany/engineering/ingenieur-in/) — for the protected *title*, not the work |

The trap: two of these look like ordinary commercial office jobs and are examined by a professional chamber instead of the IHK. Sending the file to the wrong body loses months.

### 3. State authorities {#3-state-authorities}

For the **licensed healthcare professions** and **teaching**, the competent body is an organ of the Bundesland — and its name differs by state:

| Body | Typically handles |
|---|---|
| **Landesprüfungsamt** | [Doctors](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/arzt-aerztin/), [MT/MTA](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/medizinische-technologin-mt-mta/), [physiotherapists](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/physiotherapeut-in/), [paramedics](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/notfallsanitaeter-in/) |
| **Bezirksregierung / Landesamt für Gesundheit** | [Nursing](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/pflegefachfrau-pflegefachmann/) and others, depending on the state |
| **Zeugnisanerkennungsstelle** | [Teachers](/jobs-in-germany/education/lehrer-in/) |
| **Kultusministerium / Landesjugendamt** | [Erzieher/in](/jobs-in-germany/education/erzieher-in/) |

### 4. ZAB and anabin — degrees, not professions {#4-zab-and-anabin--degrees-not-professions}

**This is the most common confusion in the whole subject.**

The **Zentralstelle für ausländisches Bildungswesen (ZAB)** and its **anabin** database assess whether a **foreign degree is comparable** to a German one. That matters for the [EU Blue Card and §18b](/jobs-in-germany/reference/visa-routes/), and for using a protected title like [Ingenieur](/jobs-in-germany/engineering/ingenieur-in/).

It is **not professional recognition.** An anabin entry does not license you to practise medicine, teach, or open a Handwerk business. Conversely, a Kammer equivalence decision is not a degree assessment. Some people need both; many need only one.

## The sixteen-state problem {#the-sixteen-state-problem}

Which bodies vary by Bundesland, and by how much:

| Varies by state | Effect |
|---|---|
| Healthcare licensing authorities | The office differs; the standard is federal, so outcomes are broadly consistent |
| **Erzieher/in** | **The law itself is state law** — training, requirements and procedure genuinely differ |
| **Teachers** | Same, and worse: pay, Verbeamtung and subject rules all differ too |
| Landwirtschaftskammern | Exist in some states only |
| Handwerkskammern, IHKs | Regional offices; IHK FOSA centralises the assessment itself |

Rule of thumb: for federally regulated professions the *office* is state-level but the *standard* is national. For **Erzieher and Lehrer, the standard itself is state law** — so pick the Bundesland before starting, not after.

## What the procedure involves {#what-the-procedure-involves}

1. **Application** to the competent body, with documents
2. **Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung** — your training compared to the German reference qualification, in content and hours
3. **A decision**: full equivalence, partial equivalence with stated gaps, or none
4. **Closing gaps** — an **Anpassungslehrgang** (supervised practice) or a **Kenntnisprüfung** (examination). Where you can choose: the course is safer, the exam faster
5. **Licence or certificate**

**EU/EEA/Swiss qualifications** in doctor, dentist, nurse, midwife, vet, pharmacist and **architect** are recognised automatically under Directive 2005/36/EC — a much shorter path, and architecture is the only non-medical profession on that list.

### Practicalities people underestimate {#practicalities-people-underestimate}

- **Sworn translations** (beglaubigte Übersetzung by a court-sworn translator) — weeks, and a few hundred euros
- **Fees** for the procedure itself, typically in the low hundreds
- **Timelines** of several months, longer where a Kenntnisprüfung is only offered twice a year
- **You can start before arriving.** Applications can be filed from abroad, and **§16d AufenthG** exists to let you enter while the procedure runs. Nursing additionally has the **Anerkennungspartnerschaft**, which allows entry at A2 German
- **The beschleunigtes Fachkräfteverfahren**, initiated by your employer, can compress processing substantially — ask them to run it

## Quick reference {#quick-reference}

| If your profession is… | Go to |
|---|---|
| A Handwerk trade | **Handwerkskammer** |
| An industrial, commercial, IT, logistics or hospitality occupation | **IHK FOSA** |
| Agriculture, forestry, horticulture | **Landwirtschaftskammer**, or the state ministry |
| A licensed healthcare profession | The **state** authority — Landesprüfungsamt or equivalent |
| Doctor or pharmacist | State authority **plus** the Kammer for the Fachsprachprüfung |
| Legal or tax clerk | **Rechtsanwalts-** or **Steuerberaterkammer**, not IHK FOSA |
| Teaching | **Zeugnisanerkennungsstelle** of your target Bundesland |
| Erzieher/in | The Bundesland's ministry or designated office — **check the state first** |
| Engineer or architect (for the title) | The **Ingenieur-** or **Architektenkammer** |
| A degree, for a visa | **ZAB / anabin** |
| A Beamtenberuf | **Nobody** — there is no procedure |
