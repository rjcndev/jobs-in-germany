This repo is written in English and deliberately keeps German terms in place, because that is how you will meet them — in job adverts, on authority websites, and on forms. Around 250 recur across the files. These are the ones that carry meaning.

**If you read only ten:** [Ausbildung](#ausbildung), [Geselle](#geselle--gesellin), [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief), [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b), [Kammer](#kammer), [Anerkennung](#anerkennung), [Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung](#gleichwertigkeitsprüfung), [Tarifvertrag](#tarifvertrag), [Beamter](#beamter--beamtin), [brutto / netto](#brutto--netto).

---

### Abschlussprüfung <a id="abschlussprüfung"></a>
The final examination of an [Ausbildung](#ausbildung). Modern occupations use a **gestreckte Abschlussprüfung** — split into Teil 1 partway through and Teil 2 at the end, with Teil 1 counting toward the final grade. See [ausbildung.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/ausbildung/).

### Altgesellenregelung {#altgesellenregelung}
**§7b HwO.** Lets a [Geselle](#geselle--gesellin) with six years in an [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b) trade — four of them in a leading position — run a business **without a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief)**. Excludes the health-related trades. The most underused route in the Handwerk. See [meister.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/).

### Anerkennung {#anerkennung}
Recognition of a foreign qualification. Mandatory before working in a regulated profession; otherwise needed only for the [visa](/jobs-in-germany/reference/visa-routes/). See [recognition-authorities.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/recognition-authorities/).

### Anerkennungspartnerschaft {#anerkennungspartnerschaft}
**§16d(3) AufenthG**, since 2024. Lets you enter Germany with **A2** German and a contract, and pursue recognition after arrival. Used mainly in [nursing](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/pflegefachfrau-pflegefachmann/).

### Anlage A / Anlage B {#anlage-a--anlage-b}
The two schedules of the [Handwerksordnung](#handwerksordnung--hwo). **Anlage A** trades require a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief) to run a business ([Meisterpflicht](#meisterpflicht)); Anlage B trades do not. Not static — twelve trades were **re-regulated in 2020**.

### Anpassungslehrgang {#anpassungslehrgang}
A supervised adaptation period, typically 6–12 months, used to close gaps found in a [Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung](#gleichwertigkeitsprüfung). The alternative is a [Kenntnisprüfung](#kenntnisprüfung) — the course is safer, the exam faster.

### Anwärter/in <a id="anwärterin"></a>
Someone in a [Vorbereitungsdienst](#vorbereitungsdienst), appointed as a **Beamter auf Widerruf** and paid **Anwärterbezüge**, well below the eventual salary.

### Approbation {#approbation}
The full, permanent licence to practise as a doctor, pharmacist, dentist or psychotherapist. Contrast [Berufserlaubnis](#berufserlaubnis).

### Arbeitszeugnis {#arbeitszeugnis}
A written reference given on leaving a job, in a coded formal register where bland phrasing carries grading. Have a German speaker read yours.

### Ausbildung {#ausbildung}
The dual vocational training system: employed by a company, schooled at a [Berufsschule](#berufsschule), 2–3½ years, paid throughout. The backbone of German qualification. See [ausbildung.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/ausbildung/).

### Auszubildende/r — Azubi {#auszubildender--azubi}
A trainee. The legal term since 1969. **[Lehrling](#lehrling)** is the historical word.

### Ausnahmebewilligung {#ausnahmebewilligung}
**§8 HwO.** A discretionary permission to run an [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b) business without a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief), on documented experience.

### Beamter / Beamtin {#beamter--beamtin}
A civil servant — appointed under public law, not employed under contract. Paid [Besoldung](#besoldung), pensioned by the state, **no right to strike**, and generally restricted to EU/EEA/Swiss nationals. See [beamte-vs-angestellte.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/beamte-vs-angestellte/).

### Beihilfe {#beihilfe}
The state's contribution to a [Beamter's](#beamter--beamtin) medical costs, commonly 50–80%. They insure only the remainder privately. A major reason Beamte net more than their gross suggests. See [health-insurance.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/health-insurance/).

### Bereitschaftsdienst {#bereitschaftsdienst}
On-call **at the workplace**, paid at a reduced rate. Distinct from [Rufbereitschaft](#rufbereitschaft).

### Berufserlaubnis {#berufserlaubnis}
A temporary, restricted permission to practise — usually limited in time, place and employer — granted while a doctor or pharmacist works toward [Approbation](#approbation).

### Berufsschule {#berufsschule}
The school half of an [Ausbildung](#ausbildung). **Taught and examined in German, without exception** — which is why the Ausbildung route requires B1–B2.

### Besoldung {#besoldung}
[Beamten](#beamter--beamtin) pay. Grades **A** (ordinary careers), **B** (senior), **R** (judges), **W** (professors), rising by **Erfahrungsstufen**. **State law since 2006**, so there are effectively seventeen systems.

### Betriebsrat {#betriebsrat}
The works council. Common in larger firms and genuinely influential over conditions, monitoring and on-call.

### brutto / netto {#brutto--netto}
Gross and net. Every figure in this repo is **brutto**. Net is commonly 60–65% of it for an employee. See [taxes-and-net-pay.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/taxes-and-net-pay/).

### Entgeltgruppe {#entgeltgruppe}
A pay grade within a [Tarifvertrag](#tarifvertrag) — E, P (nursing), S (social and education), N (paramedics). Combined with a **Stufe** (experience step) it fixes your salary.

### Fachrichtung / Schwerpunkt / Einsatzgebiet {#fachrichtung--schwerpunkt--einsatzgebiet}
Specialisations within an occupation. A **Fachrichtung** is certified and printed on your qualification; an **Einsatzgebiet** is defined by your employer and is not. The difference matters — compare [Zerspanungs-](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/zerspanungsmechaniker-in/) and [Industriemechaniker](/jobs-in-germany/industrial/industriemechaniker-in/).

### Fachsprachprüfung <a id="fachsprachprüfung"></a>
A profession-specific German exam at the relevant [Kammer](#kammer), required of third-country [doctors](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/arzt-aerztin/) and [pharmacists](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/apotheker-in/). **The usual failure point** — not clinical knowledge.

### Geselle / Gesellin {#geselle--gesellin}
A qualified tradesperson who has passed the **Gesellenprüfung**, the journeyman's examination. The rung below [Meister](#meisterbrief).

### Gleichwertigkeitsprüfung <a id="gleichwertigkeitsprüfung"></a>
The comparison of your foreign training against the German reference qualification. Outcome: full equivalence, partial with stated gaps, or none. (**Gleichwertigkeitsfeststellung** is the same procedure for unregulated occupations.)

### Handwerk {#handwerk}
The skilled trades, regulated by the [Handwerksordnung](#handwerksordnung--hwo) through the **Handwerkskammer (HWK)**. Distinct from industry, which answers to the [IHK](#ihk).

### Handwerksordnung — HwO {#handwerksordnung--hwo}
The law governing the trades, including [Anlage A / B](#anlage-a--anlage-b) and the [Handwerksrolle](#handwerksrolle).

### Handwerksrolle {#handwerksrolle}
The register of Handwerk businesses. Entry is required to run one, and normally means a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief) — or [§7b](#altgesellenregelung), [§8](#ausnahmebewilligung), §9 for EU nationals, or an employed Betriebsleiter.

### IHK {#ihk}
**Industrie- und Handelskammer** — the chamber for industrial, commercial, IT, logistics and hospitality occupations. **IHK FOSA** handles foreign-qualification equivalence for them.

### Kammer {#kammer}
A self-governing chamber: [IHK](#ihk), Handwerkskammer, Landwirtschaftskammer, and the professional chambers of doctors, pharmacists, lawyers, tax advisers, architects and engineers. **Which one is competent for you decides where your recognition file goes.** See [recognition-authorities.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/recognition-authorities/).

### Kenntnisprüfung <a id="kenntnisprüfung"></a>
An examination used instead of an [Anpassungslehrgang](#anpassungslehrgang) to close recognition gaps. Faster, harder, and sometimes offered only twice a year.

### Kirchensteuer {#kirchensteuer}
Church tax, 8–9% **of your income tax**, payable if you register as a member of a tax-collecting religious community — including on the **Anmeldung** form in your first week. See [taxes-and-net-pay.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/taxes-and-net-pay/).

### Kündigungsfrist <a id="kündigungsfrist"></a>
Notice period. Long by international standards, and symmetrical.

### Lehrling {#lehrling}
Historical German word for an [Azubi](#auszubildender--azubi). Still heard in the Handwerk, still standard in Austria, not the legal term.

### Meisterbrief {#meisterbrief}
The master craftsman's qualification. Four parts, including **Teil IV, the AEVO**, which licenses you to train apprentices. Sits at **DQR level 6 — the same as a Bachelor** — and grants university entrance without Abitur. See [meister.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/).

### Meisterpflicht {#meisterpflicht}
The requirement to hold a [Meisterbrief](#meisterbrief) to run a business in an [Anlage A](#anlage-a--anlage-b) trade.

### Mindestlohn {#mindestlohn}
The statutory minimum wage. Some sectors have a **higher branch minimum** declared under the **AEntG** — construction, security, building cleaning.

### Probezeit {#probezeit}
Probation. One to four months in an [Ausbildung](#ausbildung), usually six in employment. After it, dismissal protection is strong.

### Rufbereitschaft {#rufbereitschaft}
On call **from home** — a standby payment plus full pay for hours actually worked.

### Scheinselbständigkeit <a id="scheinselbständigkeit"></a>
Bogus self-employment: working as a contractor while in substance an employee. The client becomes liable for back social contributions. Relevant to [software contracting](/jobs-in-germany/it/softwareentwickler-in/) and [Stuhlmiete](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/friseur-in/). Enforced by the [FKS](/jobs-in-germany/public-service/zollbeamte-r/).

### Sozialabgaben {#sozialabgaben}
Social contributions — pension, unemployment, health, care. Roughly 20–21% of gross, **capped**, and matched by your employer.

### Steuerklasse {#steuerklasse}
Tax class I–VI, determining monthly withholding rather than total tax owed. The **III/V** combination distorts Elterngeld and Arbeitslosengeld, which are computed on net pay.

### Tarifvertrag {#tarifvertrag}
A collective agreement. Which one applies — TVöD, TV-L, IG Metall, DEHOGA, or none — is frequently worth 20–30% of the package. See [pay.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/pay/).

### Unterrichtung {#unterrichtung}
A 40-hour instruction course, notably the basic **§34a** requirement for [security work](/jobs-in-germany/security/). **Not a qualification** for visa purposes.

### Verbeamtung {#verbeamtung}
Appointment as a [Beamter](#beamter--beamtin). Gated by citizenship, age and an **amtsärztliche Untersuchung**.

### Vorbereitungsdienst {#vorbereitungsdienst}
The paid preparatory service before full appointment as a [Beamter](#beamter--beamtin) — the Referendariat for teachers, the police Bachelor, the customs training.

### Westbalkanregelung {#westbalkanregelung}
**§26(2) BeschV.** Lets nationals of six Western Balkan states take **any** job, qualified or not, subject to an annual quota. Widely used in hospitality, logistics and construction.

### Zeitzuschläge <a id="zeitzuschläge"></a>
Hourly supplements for unsocial hours — night 20%, Sunday 25%, holidays up to 135% under TVöD, **largely tax-free** under §3b EStG. See [shift-work-and-supplements.md](/jobs-in-germany/reference/shift-work-and-supplements/).

### Zusatzbeitrag {#zusatzbeitrag}
The supplementary health-insurance contribution set by each individual Krankenkasse, on top of the 14.6% base rate.
