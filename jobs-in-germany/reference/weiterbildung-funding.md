**Bildungsgutschein** and **Aufstiegs-BAföG** recur through this repo without ever being explained, and two further instruments that matter just as much appear nowhere at all. Collected here, because the repo has a habit it should not have: several files name a career problem — automation, a shrinking branch network, an industry converting to electric drivetrains — and none of them says that there is a **funded** way out.

The Meisterbrief's own funding detail stays in [the Meister file](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/); the general mechanics are here.

## The five instruments {#the-five-instruments}

| Instrument | Who it is for | What it pays | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bildungsgutschein** | Unemployed, or at risk of becoming so | **Course fees in full**, plus travel, childcare, materials | Agentur für Arbeit / Jobcenter — **discretionary** |
| **§82 SGB III funding** | **People in work**, at any age | A share of fees, and a wage subsidy during training | Agentur, with the employer |
| **Qualifizierungsgeld** | People in work in a **structurally changing** sector | **Wage replacement** while you retrain and stay employed | Agentur, on an employer agreement |
| **Aufstiegs-BAföG (AFBG)** | Anyone going up the ladder: Meister, Techniker, Fachwirt, Betriebswirt, Erzieher | Fee contribution + maintenance, partly **non-repayable** | BAföG office of the Bundesland |
| **Bildungsurlaub / Bildungszeit** | Employees, in most Bundesländer | **Paid leave** — not the course | State law; you notify the employer |

### Bildungsgutschein — the workhorse {#bildungsgutschein--the-workhorse}

Issued under **§81 SGB III** for *Förderung der beruflichen Weiterbildung*. It is a voucher: the Agentur decides you qualify, states what it will fund, and you redeem it at a certified provider (**AZAV**-accredited — an uncertified course cannot be paid for with one, however good it is).

It covers the full course fee plus travel, childcare and materials, and if you are receiving ALG I it continues throughout. This is how most retraining in Germany is actually paid for — including a **full second Umschulung**, typically compressed to two years, and including most of the [short tickets](/jobs-in-germany/reference/occupational-certificates/) in the certificate table.

**It is discretionary.** There is no entitlement. The decision turns on whether the training plausibly ends unemployment, so arrive with a target occupation on a shortage list, a provider, and ideally an employer's letter of interest. Ask *before* signing up for anything: approval must precede the course.

### §82 SGB III — funding while employed {#82-sgb-iii--funding-while-employed}

The **Qualifizierungschancengesetz** (2019) opened Weiterbildung funding to people who are *not* unemployed, with no age or prior-qualification restriction. The state pays a share of the course fee and a share of the wages for the hours spent training; the employer's own share falls as company size falls, so it is most generous in small firms.

It is designed for exactly the situation several files in this repo describe — a job that still exists but is visibly narrowing — and it is badly under-used because it has to be initiated with the employer rather than alone.

### Qualifizierungsgeld — the newest one {#qualifizierungsgeld--the-newest-one}

In force since **2024** under the Weiterbildungsgesetz. Where a whole workforce needs retraining because the business is structurally changing, and the employer commits to **keeping the staff**, Qualifizierungsgeld pays wage replacement (at the Kurzarbeitergeld rate — 60%, or 67% with a child) during training.

Conditions: a substantial share of the workforce affected, a **works agreement or collective agreement** underpinning it, and a certified course. It is a collective instrument, not an individual application — which is the point. It closes the loop the repo opens in [Büromanagement](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/kaufmann-frau-fuer-bueromanagement/) (automation exposure), [Bankkaufmann](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/bankkaufmann-frau/) (a shrinking branch network) and [Kfz-Mechatroniker](/jobs-in-germany/skilled-trades/kraftfahrzeugmechatroniker-in/) (the EV transition): the answer to "my sector is shrinking under me" is not only to leave.

### Aufstiegs-BAföG — for going up, not sideways <a id="aufstiegs-bafög--for-going-up-not-sideways"></a>

Funds the **Fortbildungen** above an Ausbildung: Meister, Techniker, Fachwirt, Betriebswirt, staatlich anerkannte/r Erzieher/in and comparable qualifications. Unlike student BAföG it does **not** depend on your parents' income, and unlike the Bildungsgutschein it is an **entitlement** where the conditions are met.

Structure:

- A **contribution to course and exam fees**, of which a substantial part is a grant and the remainder an interest-free **KfW loan**.
- **Passing the exam cancels a large share of the outstanding loan.** Starting a business afterwards can cancel more.
- **Full-time** students additionally receive a maintenance grant, with supplements for children and single parents.

The Meisterprämien some Bundesländer pay on top — a cash bonus for passing — sit in [the Meister file](/jobs-in-germany/reference/meister/) and in [choosing a Bundesland](/jobs-in-germany/reference/bundeslaender/), since they are state money and the amounts differ sharply.

### Bildungsurlaub / Bildungszeit — the one nobody claims {#bildungsurlaub--bildungszeit--the-one-nobody-claims}

**Statutory paid educational leave**, typically **five working days a year** (often accumulable over two years), for a recognised course. You keep your salary; the course fee is yours unless something else above covers it.

It is **state law**, so this is another **sixteen-system case** for [choosing a Bundesland](/jobs-in-germany/reference/bundeslaender/): most Bundesländer have it, a couple have never introduced it, and the qualifying periods, eligible course types and notice rules differ in every one that does. Language courses and political education commonly qualify alongside vocational ones — which makes it directly useful to arriving workers.

Take-up sits in the low single-digit percentages. It is the cheapest unclaimed entitlement in this repo.

## Two more worth knowing {#two-more-worth-knowing}

- **Weiterbildungsstipendium** — for people who finished an Ausbildung young and with a strong result: several thousand euro over three years for professional development. Administered by the **SBB**; applications go through the examining Kammer.
- **Aufstiegsstipendium** — for people with vocational qualifications and work experience going on to a **first degree**. The route from a trade into university, with money attached.

And the fallback: **Weiterbildung costs are tax-deductible** as Werbungskosten where they relate to your current or intended occupation. See [tax and net pay](/jobs-in-germany/reference/taxes-and-net-pay/).

## If you are foreign-trained {#if-you-are-foreign-trained}

- **The Bildungsgutschein also funds recognition costs and gap courses** — an Anpassungslehrgang or Kenntnisprüfung preparation is fundable, as is the [Berufssprachkurs](/jobs-in-germany/reference/language-certificates/) alongside it.
- **Aufstiegs-BAföG requires a residence status with settled prospects** — check eligibility against your own permit before planning around it.
- Both are decided by German offices in German. Budget time for the paperwork.
