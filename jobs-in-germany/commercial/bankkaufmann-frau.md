## What is actually regulated {#what-is-actually-regulated}

Anyone can be employed as a Bankkaufmann/-frau. But if you give **investment advice**, you
personally must be:

- **demonstrably competent** (Sachkundenachweis), and
- **registered with BaFin** in the employee register, under **§87 WpHG** and the
  **Mitarbeiteranzeigenverordnung (MaAnzV)**

The same applies to sales staff and compliance officers. Complaints against you are
recorded in that register. Outside banks, insurance intermediation needs **§34d GewO** and
financial investment brokerage **§34f GewO**.

This is the [Berufskraftfahrer](/jobs-in-germany/logistics/berufskraftfahrer-in/) pattern in a suit:
the job title is unprotected, and the thing that gates the work sits somewhere else
entirely. Check what is being controlled, not what it is called.

Separately, **Geldwäschegesetz (GwG)** obligations apply to the whole sector, and a clean
**Führungszeugnis** is a practical precondition for employment.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

Account and payment services, consumer and mortgage lending (Kreditgeschäft), securities
and investment advice (Wertpapierberatung), and cross-selling of building-society and
insurance products. Specialisation follows quickly: **Privatkunden** (retail),
**Firmenkunden** (corporate — the best-paid advisory track), **Kredit**, **Wertpapier**,
or **Compliance** and back office.

Increasingly the branch-facing part of the job is advisory and sales; routine transactions
have moved to apps and self-service.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3 years dual**, bank plus Berufsschule
- **Entry:** mittlerer Schulabschluss or Abitur; **Abitur is the norm** — banking is among
  the most credential-competitive Ausbildungen
- **Paid throughout:** roughly €1,150–1,400/month gross, among the better-paying trainings
- **Ends with** the IHK Abschlussprüfung
- **Ladder:** **Bankfachwirt/in**, then **Bankbetriebswirt/in**, or a part-time Bachelor.
  Banks fund these readily; they are the standard route to advisory and management grades.

## Pay {#pay}

Three different tariff worlds, and which one you are in matters more than your performance:


<div class="jig-table" markdown="1">

| Setting | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| **Sparkasse (TVöD-S, typically EG 6–9a)** | **€3,240 – €4,980** |
| Private bank (AGV Banken tariff), entry | €3,200 – €3,800 |
| Private bank, experienced adviser | €3,900 – €5,000 |
| Firmenkundenbetreuer / specialist | €4,500 – €6,500 |
| Branch or team management | €5,500 – €8,000 |

</div>


The **Sparkassen row is tariff-verified**: TVöD-S shares the TVöD VKA scale, table valid
**01.05.2026 – 31.03.2027**, checked 2026-09. Eingruppierung varies by role and institution,
so treat EG 6–9a as the usual band rather than a rule. Sparkassen also pay a
**Sparkassensonderzahlung** on top.

The private-bank and specialist rows are **market estimates**. Genossenschaftsbanken
(Volksbanken/Raiffeisenbanken) run their own agreements at broadly comparable levels.
Investment banking and asset management sit outside all of this and pay on a different
scale entirely.

## Demand and outlook — read this honestly {#demand-and-outlook--read-this-honestly}

The sector is **shrinking**. Branch closures have run for a decade, retail headcount is
down substantially, and routine transaction work has largely gone to apps. Bankkaufmann/-frau
is not a shortage occupation in the way nursing or driving are.

That does not make the qualification a bad bet — but it changes what a good bet looks like
inside it. Demand is solid and growing in **corporate banking, credit analysis, compliance,
anti-money-laundering and regulatory reporting**; it is weak in branch retail service. The
qualification is a strong foundation; a career planned around a branch counter is not.

## Foreign candidates {#foreign-candidates}

1. **To work:** no recognition legally required for the title.
2. **To advise:** you need the **Sachkundenachweis** and **BaFin registration**. A foreign
   banking qualification does not automatically satisfy this, and German product, tax and
   regulatory knowledge is the substance of it.
3. **For the visa:** **IHK FOSA** equivalence assessment. A foreign finance degree may
   instead be assessed via **anabin**, which can open the **EU Blue Card** at specialist
   and corporate-banking salary levels.
4. **Language: C1.** Advisory conversations carry legal weight and are documented; the
   compliance paperwork is dense. English matters in corporate and investment banking,
   not in retail.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Sparkasse vs. private vs. genossenschaftlich** determines pay, culture and security.
  Understand which you are applying to — they are not interchangeable.
- **Sales targets** in retail advisory are real and have driven repeated mis-selling
  controversies. Ask how performance is measured before signing.
- **BaFin registration follows you.** Complaints are recorded against your entry.
- **Branch retail is the shrinking end** of a shrinking sector. Specialise early.
