Profession profiles in this category.

<div class="jig-cards"></div>

- [Bankkaufmann/-frau (Bank Clerk)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/bankkaufmann-frau/) <span>Retail and commercial banking: accounts, payments, lending and investment advice.</span>
- [Immobilienkaufmann/-frau (Real Estate Manager / Agent)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/immobilienkaufmann-frau/) <span>Another activity-gated occupation, like Bankkaufmann: the title is free, but brokering property, developing it or managing residential property for others all require a §34c GewO permit — with a continuing training obligation attached.</span>
- [Industriekaufmann/-frau (Industrial Clerk)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/industriekaufmann-frau/) <span>The commercial backbone of German manufacturing: purchasing, production planning, sales, controlling and HR inside industrial firms.</span>
- [Kaufmann/-frau für Büromanagement (Office Management Clerk)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/kaufmann-frau-fuer-bueromanagement/) <span>Germany's single most common Ausbildung.</span>
- [Kaufmann/-frau für Versicherungen und Finanzanlagen (Insurance and Financial Services Agent)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/kaufmann-frau-versicherungen-finanzanlagen/) <span>Completes the finance-sector permit picture.</span>
- [Notar/in (Civil-Law Notary)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/notar-in/) <span>Genuinely unlike anything else in this repo.</span>
- [Rechtsanwaltsfachangestellte/r (Legal Secretary / Paralegal)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/rechtsanwaltsfachangestellte-r/) <span>Runs a law firm's procedural machinery: deadlines, files, court filings and fee billing.</span>
- [Steuerfachangestellte/r (Tax Clerk)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/steuerfachangestellte-r/) <span>Prepares bookkeeping, payroll and tax returns in a Steuerkanzlei.</span>
- [Wirtschaftsprüfer/in (Statutory Auditor)](/jobs-in-germany/commercial/wirtschaftspruefer-in/) <span>A reserved activity under the WPO — only a Wirtschaftsprüfer may sign a statutory audit opinion — behind an examination with a worse pass rate than the Steuerberater one.</span>
