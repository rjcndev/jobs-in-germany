## Why one file {#why-one-file}

The repo's rule is a file per profession, and these two get one between them because a separate file for each would repeat the same seven sections with the clinical content swapped. What differs is the work; what matters for choosing, qualifying, immigrating and getting paid is shared:

- both are **Heilmittelerbringer** — therapies prescribed by a doctor and billed to health insurance under the Heilmittel-Richtlinie
- both are **regulated with a state licence** and **no automatic EU recognition**
- both train for **three years in a Berufsfachschule**, not a university and not a paid dual Ausbildung
- both sit on the same unresolved academisation question
- both pay similarly, and similarly poorly

Where they genuinely diverge, it is marked below.

## What the work is {#what-the-work-is}

**Logopädie** — speech, language, voice and swallowing.

- **Dysphagie** — swallowing disorders after stroke, in neurodegenerative disease, in intensive care. The most clinically serious part of the field: aspiration kills people
- **Aphasie, Dysarthrie** — language and speech after neurological injury
- **Kindersprache** — developmental language disorders, articulation, stuttering, cleft palate, hearing-related language development
- **Stimme** — voice therapy, including for teachers and singers, and post-laryngectomy
- Trachealkanülenmanagement in clinical settings

**Ergotherapie** — restoring the ability to act, in whatever the person's life requires.

- **Neurologie** — after stroke and brain injury: motor function, perception, everyday tasks
- **Geriatrie** — maintaining independence, falls, dementia
- **Pädiatrie** — sensory integration, motor development, school readiness
- **Psychiatrie** — daily structure, work capacity, group therapy. A large field, and the one most often forgotten
- **Handtherapie** — post-surgical and post-trauma rehabilitation of the hand, frequently in cooperation with surgeons
- **Arbeitsplatzanpassung** and assistive equipment

## Confusable titles {#confusable-titles}

| Title | Reality |
|---|---|
| **Sprachtherapeut/in** | A wider, partly academic group — **Klinische Linguistik**, **Sprachheilpädagogik**, **Patholinguistik**. They may obtain the same insurance billing approval and do the same work, from a **degree** rather than the Logopädie school route |
| **Atem-, Sprech- und Stimmlehrer/in** | Voice and speech training, not a medical profession |
| **[Physiotherapeut/in](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/physiotherapeut-in/)** | The third Heilmittel profession. Same structure, different body |
| **Ergotherapie vs. Arbeitstherapie** | Arbeitstherapie is one field *within* Ergotherapie, not a separate qualification |
| **"Therapeut"** alone | Unprotected. Anyone may use it |

That first row matters more than it looks: **Logopädie is one of the few German professions reachable by two structurally different routes**, one vocational and one academic, ending at the same practice.

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **3 years** at a state-approved Berufsfachschule, theory plus supervised treatment in the school's own outpatient clinic
- **Entry:** mittlerer Schulabschluss; for Logopädie, most schools test hearing, voice and speech, and a phoniatric certificate of fitness is commonly required
- **Staatliche Prüfung**, then the **Erlaubnis** from the state authority
- **Bachelor routes exist** under the Modellklauseln in both professions' statutes — primärqualifizierend, ending in the same Erlaubnis plus a degree. The clauses have been extended repeatedly rather than resolved
- **Above it:** practice ownership, clinical specialisation, Lehrlogopädie and school teaching, academic routes into research and therapy science

### Schulgeld — check before you enrol {#schulgeld--check-before-you-enrol}

These professions were notorious for **charging students tuition** at private schools — several hundred euro a month for a three-year training leading to modest pay, a combination that has no parallel in the paid dual system described in [Ausbildung](/jobs-in-germany/reference/ausbildung/).

Since 2019 the Bundesländer have moved to abolish Schulgeld for the therapy professions, and most have. **It is state law, so it is another sixteen-system question** — see [choosing a Bundesland](/jobs-in-germany/reference/bundeslaender/) — and some private schools remain outside the arrangements. Establish for your state and your school what you will pay and whether any training allowance is paid, before signing anything.

## The certificate treadmill {#the-certificate-treadmill}

Both professions run on post-qualification certificates that are **billing permissions as much as skills** — they decide what a practice may invoice and at what rate.

| Logopädie | Ergotherapie |
|---|---|
| LSVT LOUD, FOTT, Castillo Morales, Trachealkanülenmanagement, Dysphagie-specific courses | Bobath, Handtherapie (DAHTH), Sensorische Integration, Affolter, psychiatric method courses |

They are **frequently self-funded**, cost hundreds to thousands of euro each, and consume unpaid leave. [Occupational certificates](/jobs-in-germany/reference/occupational-certificates/) collects the pattern and contrasts it with the employer-funded safety tickets elsewhere in this repo. It is a real, continuing, personally-borne cost that the pay table cannot show — and it is worth asking an employer to fund it, because some do.

## Pay {#pay}

**Market estimate**, except the public-sector row.

| Setting | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Entry, private practice | €2,600 – €3,000 |
| Experienced, private practice | €3,000 – €3,600 |
| With sought-after certificates (Dysphagie, Handtherapie, Neuro) | €3,300 – €4,000 |
| **Clinic / rehab / public sector, TVöD EG 7–9a** | **€3,295 – €4,980** |
| Practice owner | Not comparable — a small business |

TVöD VKA row verified for this repo, valid **01.05.2026 – 31.03.2027**.

One genuine improvement worth recording: **Heilmittel prices were substantially raised from 2019**, when the TSVG removed the link to regional price ceilings and moved to uniform, higher national prices. Practice revenue rose materially and some of it reached wages. Pay in these professions is better than it was a decade ago — while still sitting below what a three-year regulated healthcare qualification earns in almost any comparable country.

## Demand and outlook {#demand-and-outlook}

**Shortage in both, with the sharpest demand in neurology and geriatrics.** An ageing population produces stroke, dysphagia and dementia caseloads that only grow, and paediatric waiting lists are long everywhere.

- **Logopädie**: dysphagia and neurological rehabilitation are the strongest fields, and clinical dysphagia work is the best-paid and hardest to fill.
- **Ergotherapie**: psychiatry and geriatrics are expanding; hand therapy is a well-paid niche with a serious certificate requirement.
- **Practice ownership is genuinely open.** Heilmittel practices need a **Kassenzulassung** based on qualification and premises standards — there is **no numerical cap** of the kind that rations [psychotherapy](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/psychotherapeut-in/) licences. Compared with almost everything else in this repo's healthcare section, the path to independence is unobstructed.
- The academisation question will eventually be decided. If it follows [midwifery](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/hebamme/), the entry bar rises to Abitur and the route becomes a paid dual degree — better for those who come after, disruptive during the change.

## Foreign-trained candidates {#foreign-trained-candidates}

1. **Recognition is mandatory**; the title and the work are both protected.
2. **No automatic EU recognition.** Individual equivalence assessment for everyone, including EU qualifications.
3. **Competent authority is the Bundesland.** Gaps close through an **Anpassungslehrgang** or **Kenntnisprüfung**. See [recognition authorities](/jobs-in-germany/reference/recognition-authorities/).
4. **Language is the real gate, especially for Logopädie.** B2 is the legal floor; the work is *in* the phonology, grammar and idiom of German, and treating a German child's articulation or an aphasic adult's word-finding requires native-level command of the target language. This is the one profession in this repo where the language is not a medium for the work but the **object** of it — be honest with yourself about it. Ergotherapie is B2–C1 and more forgiving.
5. **Visa:** §18a, or §16d to pursue recognition. **No Blue Card.**
6. **Method certificates do not transfer** and will be re-sat here.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Check the Schulgeld position for your Bundesland and your school** before enrolling.
- **Logopädie has a second, academic route** — if you already hold a linguistics or speech science degree, ask about it rather than starting a three-year school training.
- **The certificates are the career**, and they are largely self-funded. Plan and negotiate for them from the start.
- **Practice ownership is reachable**, unusually for healthcare here. If independence is the goal, this is one of the few fields where regulation is not what stands in the way.
- **The academisation is unresolved.** A Bachelor route may be better or worse for you depending on the state and the school — it is not automatically the higher-status choice yet.
