> Dispenses, advises and compounds in a pharmacy, under a pharmacist's supervision — **reformed in 2023** to loosen that supervision for experienced staff. Sits under [Apotheker](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/apotheker-in/) the way [MFA](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/medizinische-fachangestellte-r/) sits under [Arzt](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/arzt-aerztin/), with one important difference: **PTA is a regulated profession with a state licence**, and MFA is not.

## The 2023 reform {#the-2023-reform}

The **PTA-Reformgesetz** took effect on 01.01.2023, replacing the old PharmTAG. What it changed:

- **Supervision was loosened.** The pharmacy owner may permit a PTA with sufficient experience — broadly, three years — to work with **reduced supervision**, rather than requiring a pharmacist's direct oversight of each act. This is the substantive change and it altered what a PTA's working day looks like.
- **The curriculum was strengthened** and the internship structure tightened.
- **The training remained largely unpaid.** This was the profession's central demand and the reform did not meet it. Two years at a Berufsfachschule, frequently with **school fees**, and only the six-month pharmacy internship is paid.

That last point is worth stating plainly because it is unusual in this repo: nearly every other qualification here is either a **[paid dual Ausbildung](/jobs-in-germany/reference/ausbildung/)** or a degree with student financing. PTA is neither. It is a school qualification you pay for, for a regulated healthcare profession that pays modestly afterwards — and it is a large part of why entrant numbers have fallen.

## What the job involves {#what-the-job-involves}

- **Abgabe und Beratung** — dispensing prescriptions, checking interactions, advising on self-medication. The bulk of the work and the reason language matters
- **Rezeptur and Defektur** — compounding individual preparations and batch stock: ointments, capsules, solutions. German pharmacies still compound, and PTA do most of it
- **Prüfung** — identity and quality testing of incoming substances
- **Abrechnung** with the statutory insurers, where a formal error voids reimbursement
- **Botendienst, Rezeptur documentation, stock and cold-chain management**
- **Impfen** is not PTA work; vaccination in pharmacies rests with pharmacists

Settings: mostly public pharmacies (**Offizin**), plus hospital pharmacies, industry and wholesale.

| **PKA — Pharmazeutisch-kaufmännische/r Angestellte/r** | The **commercial** assistant: ordering, stock, accounts. A dual Ausbildung, **not** a regulated profession, and not permitted to dispense |
| **Apothekenhelfer/in** | The historical name for what is now PKA |
| **[MFA](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/medizinische-fachangestellte-r/)** | The doctor's-practice equivalent. Unregulated, examined by the Ärztekammer, different work entirely |

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

- **2 years** at a state-approved **PTA-Schule** — theory, practical pharmacy, chemistry, botany, galenics. Often fee-paying, generally unpaid
- **6 months' Praktikum** in a pharmacy, paid at a modest rate set in the sector agreement
- **Staatliche Prüfung**, then the **Erlaubnis** from the state authority
- **Entry requirement:** mittlerer Schulabschluss
- **Above it:** Fachweiterbildungen (Onkologie, Ernährungsberatung, Dermokosmetik, Homöopathie), hospital-pharmacy specialisation, or — the significant one — **studying Pharmazie to become a pharmacist**, for which the PTA qualification confers no exemption but does confer a good reason to be sure

## Pay {#pay}

Covered by the **ADEXA/ADA Gehaltstarifvertrag** — the same agreement verified for [Apotheker](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/apotheker-in/) in this repo, valid **01.01.2026 – 31.12.2026** and **not applying in Nordrhein or Sachsen**, which negotiate separately.

**The PTA salary groups were not separately verified for this repo — treat the figures below as indicative, not exact**, and check the current ADEXA table before relying on them.

| Stage | Gross/month |
|---|---|
| Internship (the 6 months) | Low — a trainee rate, not a wage |
| Entry, newly licensed | €2,600 – €2,900 |
| After several Berufsjahre | €3,000 – €3,600 |
| Experienced with Weiterbildung, or leading Rezeptur | €3,400 – €4,000 |
| Hospital pharmacy under **TVöD EG 6–9a** | **€3,240 – €4,980** |

The hospital row is the verified TVöD VKA scale, valid **01.05.2026 – 31.03.2027**, and — as with [MFA](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/medizinische-fachangestellte-r/) — the public-sector employer is frequently the better-paying one for comparable work.

Against the pharmacist's **€4,166 rising to €4,922 after eleven years**, the shape of the sector is clear: neither role is well paid for its responsibility, and the gap between them is narrower than five years of university would suggest.

## Demand and outlook {#demand-and-outlook}

**Shortage of PTA, in a sector that is itself shrinking.** Both halves are true and they pull in opposite directions.

- **Pharmacies cannot find PTA.** Entrant numbers fell — unpaid training in a cost-of-living squeeze is a poor offer — and the shortage is acute.
- **But the number of pharmacies is falling.** Germany has lost pharmacies steadily for years, squeezed by fixed dispensing fees, mail-order competition and succession failures. A shortage of staff inside a contracting sector does not reliably produce rising pay.
- **The reform direction matters to you personally.** Proposals to permit branches led by a PTA rather than a present pharmacist recur, are strongly opposed by the pharmacists' organisations, and would substantially expand PTA responsibility — see [Apotheker](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/apotheker-in/). Whether the pay would follow is the open question.

## Foreign-trained candidates {#foreign-trained-candidates}

1. **Recognition is mandatory.** This is a regulated profession; you may not do the work without the state Erlaubnis.
2. **No automatic EU recognition** — PTA is not on the Directive's list of seven, so even EU qualifications are assessed individually.
3. **Competent authority is the Bundesland**, and procedures differ. See [recognition authorities](/jobs-in-germany/reference/recognition-authorities/).
4. **Expect gaps to be found in German-specific content**: Rezeptur practice, Apothekenbetriebsordnung, and insurance billing. An **Anpassungslehrgang** is the usual remedy.
5. **Language: B2 legally, B2–C1 realistically.** Dispensing advice is patient-facing, and getting a dose instruction wrong is a safety event.
6. **Visa:** §18a, or §16d to pursue recognition. **No Blue Card** — well below the threshold.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **The training is not paid and may cost you fees.** Budget for two years of it. This is the single biggest difference between PTA and almost every other qualification in this repo.
- **PTA is not a shortcut to pharmacist.** It grants no credit toward the degree.
- **Check whether your Bundesland is inside the ADEXA/ADA agreement.** Nordrhein and Sachsen are not.
- **Reduced supervision is a permission, not a right.** Whether you get it depends on the pharmacy owner, and it should be discussed before you accept a post.
- **Rezeptur skill is worth money.** It is the part of the job least amenable to automation or mail order, and the part where hospital pharmacies compete for you.
