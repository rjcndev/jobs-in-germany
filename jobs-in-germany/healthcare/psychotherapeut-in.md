## The 2020 reform {#the-2020-reform}

The **new Psychotherapeutengesetz** took effect on 01.09.2020 and inverted the route.

| | **Before 2020** | **Since 2020** |
|---|---|---|
| Degree | Psychology Diplom/Master, or an education degree for the KJP route | **Polyvalent Bachelor + Master** in Klinische Psychologie und Psychotherapie |
| Then | **3–5 years of post-graduate Ausbildung** at a private Institut | Approbation **at the end of the Master** |
| Paid? | **Barely, or not at all** — the practical year in a clinic was notoriously paid a few hundred euro a month, while the training itself cost €20,000–€30,000 in fees | Normal student status, then **paid employment** |
| Approbation at | The end of that training | The end of the Master |
| Specialisation | Built into the route | **Weiterbildung afterwards**, 5 years, **paid** |

The old *Psychotherapeuten in Ausbildung* (PiA) arrangement was a national scandal inside the profession: qualified psychologists doing clinical work for pocket money while paying an institute for the privilege. The reform ended it by moving the licence forward and the specialisation back — the same structural move medicine made long ago, where Approbation comes first and Facharzt training follows as paid employment.

**Transitional arrangements run into the 2030s**, so both routes exist in the workforce and job ads may still refer to either.

## The Kassensitz — the constraint that shapes the career {#the-kassensitz--the-constraint-that-shapes-the-career}

Approbation lets you treat patients. It does **not** let you bill the statutory health insurers, and roughly nine in ten people in Germany are statutorily insured.

For that you need a **Zulassung als Vertragspsychotherapeut**, and the number of them is **capped per planning region** under the **Bedarfsplanung** of the Kassenärztliche Vereinigung. In practice every attractive region is "overserved" on paper and closed to new licences.

So a licence only becomes free when someone gives one up — and the incoming practitioner "takes over the practice", paying the outgoing one. The price is nominally for the practice (patient records, fittings, goodwill); in substance it is the scarcity value of the licence. **Five figures is normal; desirable cities run higher.** It is one of the few places in German professional life where a state permission is, in effect, bought and sold.

The consequences are visible from the patient side as well: waiting times for a statutory therapy place run to months, while a psychotherapist cannot simply open a practice to meet the demand.

**The ways around it, all imperfect:**

| Route | Reality |
|---|---|
| **Privatpraxis** | Open immediately, no cap. Patients are self-paying or privately insured — a much smaller market, unevenly distributed |
| **Kostenerstattungsverfahren** | A statutory patient who can document that no licensed place is available may have private treatment reimbursed. Case-by-case, contested, and administratively heavy |
| **Angestellt in einer Praxis or MVZ** | Work under someone else's licence. Increasingly common, and the realistic first job |
| **Clinic employment** | Psychiatric, psychosomatic and rehab hospitals. Tariff pay, no licence problem |
| **Half a Sitz** | Licences can be split; a half-Sitz is cheaper and widely used |

## Confusable titles {#confusable-titles}

| Title | What it actually is |
|---|---|
| **Psychologe/Psychologin** | Someone with a psychology degree. **Not** a therapist, may not treat, and the word itself is not protected the way the therapy titles are |
| **Psychiater/in** | A **doctor** — see [Arzt](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/arzt-aerztin/) — with a Facharzt in psychiatry. May prescribe medication, which a psychotherapist may not |
| **Heilpraktiker/in für Psychotherapie** | A real and legal permit, obtained by passing a **Gesundheitsamt examination** with **no degree required**. May treat privately; may not bill statutory insurance and may not use the protected title. The single most common source of confusion here |
| **Systemischer Coach, Berater, Therapeut (non-medical)** | Unregulated words. Anyone may use most of them |

## Qualification route {#qualification-route}

1. **Bachelor** (6 semesters) — must be the *polyvalent* psychology Bachelor containing the legally specified clinical content. **An ordinary psychology Bachelor does not qualify you to enter the Master**, which catches people who chose a programme before checking.
2. **Master** in Klinische Psychologie und Psychotherapie (4 semesters), ending in the **staatliche Prüfung** and **Approbation**. Places are heavily oversubscribed.
3. **Weiterbildung**, 5 years full-time equivalent, in an approved institution, leading to **Fachpsychotherapeut/in** in an area (adults; children and adolescents; neuropsychology) and a method (Verhaltenstherapie, tiefenpsychologisch fundierte Psychotherapie, analytische Psychotherapie, systemische Therapie). **This is paid employment.**
4. The **Fachkunde** from that Weiterbildung is what a Kassensitz requires.

## Pay {#pay}

Employed positions are tariff-bound and verifiable; independent practice is not.

| Stage | Setting | Gross/month |
|---|---|---|
| Weiterbildung, employed | Clinic or Institutsambulanz | **Market estimate** €3,600 – €4,500 |
| Approbiert, employed | Hospital, TVöD/TV-L **EG 13–14** | €4,901 – €7,552 |
| Employed in a practice or MVZ | Private employer | **Market estimate** €3,800 – €5,500 |
| **Own Kassensitz** | Self-employed | **Market estimate**: practice revenue €120,000 – €200,000+, before roughly 30–40% costs |
| Privatpraxis | Self-employed | Highly variable; depends entirely on catchment |

The EG 13–14 figures reuse the scale already verified for [Apotheker](/jobs-in-germany/healthcare/apotheker-in/) in hospital employment, valid **01.05.2026 – 31.03.2027**.

Two honest qualifications. A Kassensitz is bought, so the headline practice income is serviced by debt for years. And the employed-in-a-practice band — where most newly approbierte therapists actually start — sits well below what the qualification suggests, because the licence holder, not the therapist, captures the scarcity value.

## Demand and outlook {#demand-and-outlook}

**Demand for treatment is far above supply, and the supply is capped by regulation rather than by the number of qualified people.** That is an unusual position: a shortage profession where more qualified entrants do not, by themselves, shorten the queue.

- Employment in clinics, rehab and MVZ is **growing and accessible**.
- Statutory outpatient licences are **rationed and expensive**.
- The Bedarfsplanung has been repeatedly criticised and partially adjusted; assume it will keep changing, and do not plan a decade around today's rules.
- Child and adolescent therapy is the most under-supplied area, and the merged qualification now routes into it through the Weiterbildung.

## Foreign-trained candidates {#foreign-trained-candidates}

1. **Approbation is mandatory** to use the title or treat under that description.
2. **No automatic EU recognition** — psychotherapy is not among the seven professions on the Directive's list. Every application is assessed individually, including EU ones.
3. **The equivalence hurdle is high and specific.** German Approbation requires a defined curriculum with clinical content; psychology degrees from most countries, including strong ones, are found to differ. Expect a **Kenntnisprüfung** or a substantial **Anpassungslehrgang**, and expect the assessment to be state-by-state.
4. **Language: C1, without qualification.** Therapy is conducted in language; nuance, idiom and register are the working instrument, not a convenience.
5. **Visa:** **§18b**; Blue Card reachable in clinic employment at EG 13–14.
6. **Kammer and [Versorgungswerk](/jobs-in-germany/reference/versorgungswerke/)** membership follow Approbation, with the pension exemption to apply for within three months.

## Pitfalls {#pitfalls}

- **Check the Bachelor before enrolling.** The wrong psychology Bachelor closes the route entirely, and this is the most common and most costly mistake in the new system.
- **Approbation is not a Kassensitz**, and the gap between them is money and years.
- **Do not confuse the Heilpraktiker-Psychotherapie permit with this profession.** It is a legitimate but much narrower licence, and it is not a route into the regulated title.
- **Transitional cohorts still exist.** If you are comparing your qualification against a German colleague's, establish which system theirs came from.
- **The Weiterbildung is now paid** — if anyone offers you unpaid clinical work as "training", that is the old system, and it is over.
